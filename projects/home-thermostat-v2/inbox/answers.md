
## Round 1
q1: One AA battery, about 1 year. Phone commands act within about 1 hour (the device checks once per hour, or at once if you press its button).
q2: No preference, use your judgment.
q3: Recommended: 230 V thermoelectric valve heads on radiators/manifold (up to about 4 heads per room) or a zone valve / boiler enable contact. We rate each output 5 A at 230 V, voltage-free contact (installer wires live to it). Small relays, fits the 3-module size.
q4: Recommended: switch heating OFF before the battery is empty, and send low-battery warnings to the app starting at least 30 days before. No extra cost.

## Round 2
q1: Recommended: standard 2-wire NTC 10k probes (the common type sold for heating controllers), cable up to 5 m, accuracy about +/-0.5 C between 10 and 30 C. About 0.5-1 USD per probe. No effect on battery life.
q2: It is just a prototype, so cheap for small units: 10 units under 25 USD each would be great.
q3: No preference, use your judgment.
q4: Cost first: accept about 10-11 months of battery life if it saves 0.5-1 USD per unit.

## Round 3
q1: The 3-module Italian box is a hard, non-negotiable constraint, so the whole-box option is out. Keep the 3-module insert with compact relays; if a cheaper compact relay that is safe for 230 V exists, prefer it. Cost near the prototype target if possible, but size comes first.
