## Stage 3 additions (2026-10-20)

- Family: TPS610993 = 3.0 V, TPS610994 = 3.3 V, TPS610995 = 3.6 V, TPS610996 = 4.5 V, TPS610997 = 5.0 V (KiCad symbol descriptions, Digikey TPS610995DRVT "Fixed 3.6V"). The architecture needs TPS610994 for the 3.3 V rail.
- Adjustable TPS61099DRVR: VFB 500 mV typ, FB bias 0.01 uA typ, ILIM 0.8 A min / 1 A typ, L 2.2-6.8 uH, Cin 10 uF, Cout 22-100 uF (search summaries of the PDF; verify).
- JLCPCB: only TPS61099DRVR C2842395 (stock 8746, 1.01 USD) is indexed; TPS610994/5 are not.
