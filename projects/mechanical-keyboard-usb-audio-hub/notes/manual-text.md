# Manual and box text (product notes)

Items the product person asked to put in the manual and on the box. Each line says where it comes from.

1. "For fast charging, use a USB-C charger rated 65 W or more. With a smaller charger, fast charging on all ports at once can overload it and the keyboard may restart." (PM round 7, q2; R9; conflicts.md C16)
2. "Without the charger, each USB port gives at least 75 mA (60 mA when recording, listening loudly and four fast USB 3 devices are in use), enough for mice, keyboards and dongles. Flash drives, SSDs and phone charging need the charger or a laptop USB-C port that gives 3 A." (PM rounds 4-5; R19, R21)
3. "Some computer ports switch off at exactly 900 mA. If the keyboard drops out of such a port with several devices plugged in, connect the charger." (PM round 7, q1; R19: the cut-off can reach about 1.13 A at its upper tolerance)
4. Hot-swap sockets are supplied by the product owner (consigned) — production note, not manual text. (PM round 4, q3)
