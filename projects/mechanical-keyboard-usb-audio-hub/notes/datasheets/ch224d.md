# CH224D (WCH PD3.0 sink, QFN-20 3x3, 0.4 mm), read 2026-10-22
Source: CH224 manual V2.1, datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/4f12eebccb4eda2c0038806df0155265.pdf (14 pages). LCSC C3975094: 14165 in stock, $0.2403 at 150, $0.2003 at 500.

CH224D pins (p3-4; 0 = exposed pad): 0 GND (EP) | 1 DRV (weak drive output for the config resistor) | 2 VBUS (supply in, 0.1 or 1 uF) | 3, 4 NC | 5 GATE (HV output for a high-side N-FET: "customized function") | 6 NMOS# (active-low enable of the NMOS drive; the reference circuit ties it to GND) | 7 VDD (internal regulator out 4.7 V, 1 uF) | 8 DP | 9 DM | 10 CC2 | 11 CC1 | 12 CFG3 | 13 CFG2 | 14 ISP | 15 ISN | 16, 17, 18 NC | 19 CFG1 | 20 NC.

Reference circuit (p9, 9 V by Rset 6.8 k from DRV/CFG1 to GND): VBUS direct on pin 2, 2 x 5.1 k Rd (CC1, CC2) to GND, 1 uF on VDD and on VBUS, NMOS# and GND tied, ISP/ISN to GND. Rset: 6.8 k = 9 V, 24 k = 12 V, 56 k = 15 V, open (NC) = 20 V. There is NO PG pin (unlike CH224A/K): no power-good output to drive a P-FET gate.

Abs max (p13, CH224D): VBUS -0.5..24 V; CC1/CC2 -0.5..20 V; VDD -0.5..6 V; DP, DM, CFG1-3, DRV, NMOS#, ISP, ISN -0.5..VDD+0.5; GATE -0.5..VIOHV+6.5; Ta -40..100 C; 300 mW. Electrical (p14): VDD out 4.6/4.7/4.8 V, 10 mA max external load, POR 2.2-2.6 V. Supply current not listed for the D.
CH224A/Q (not chosen): VHV/VBUS/CC 32 V, PG pin, I2C 0x22/0x23, DFN10/ESSOP10. CH224K: VBUS 13.5 V (unusable).
