# SN74LVC1G123 (TI SCES586E, March 2024): read by delta review 4

Source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g123.pdf (read as text by hw-reviewer, 2026-10-23).

- Pins (DCT / SM8): A = 1, B = 2, CLR = 3, GND = 4, Q = 5, Cext = 6, Rext/Cext = 7, VCC = 8 (p.3). Matches 74xGxx:74LVC1G123 and SSOP-8 pads 1-8.
- VCC 1.65-5.5 V; VCC and VI abs max 6.5 V; recommended VI 0-5.5 V (p.4).
- Inputs: VIH >= 0.7 VCC, VIL <= 0.3 VCC (p.4 sec 5.3). No VT+/VT- table: the trigger qualification is computed with 0.3-0.7 VCC bounds. A and B are Schmitt; CLR is not (p.12); slow edges allowed on A and B.
- II +-1 uA; Rext/Cext leakage +-0.25 uA; Ioff +-10 uA, -40 to 125 C (p.5).
- Rext >= 1 k at VCC >= 3 V (p.5). K is given only as a typical curve (Fig 5-6); pulse-width curves go to 1 uF and 200 k (Fig 5-5); a 100 uF Cext appears in the timing table (p.6). Pulse width with 120 k / 22 uF (18.7 uF biased) ~2.2 s, K tolerance assumed 0.9-1.1: measure on the first board.
- Glitch-free power-up reset (p.12). Function table (p.13): triggers on A falling with B high, on B rising with A low, and on CLR rising; edge-triggered, retriggerable.
- Lowest-rated part on VBUS_HOST: 6.5 V abs max vs SMBJ5.0A breakdown 6.4-7.0 V; fast transients filtered by R685/C663 (10 us). A sustained host overvoltage of 6.5-7 V is outside the rating (no requirement covers it).
