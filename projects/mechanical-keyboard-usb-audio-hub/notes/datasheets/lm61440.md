# LM61440 (TI, 36 V 4 A sync buck) - facts for the design
Source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm61440.pdf (read via text proxy, pages as cited; only first 100k chars read)
- VIN 3.0-36 V operating, 42 V abs max (p5). Iout 0-4 A. Package VQFN-HR 14 pin 4.0x3.5 mm (RJR) (p1, p4-5).
- Pins: 1 BIAS, 2 VCC, 3 AGND, 4 FB, 5 PGOOD, 6 RT, 7 EN/SYNC, 8+12 VIN1/2, 9+11 PGND1/2, 10 SW, 13 RBOOT, 14 CBOOT.
- Rds_on high 41 mohm typ / 82 max, low 21 / 45 mohm (p7). Current limit high-side 6-8.1 A, low-side 4-5.4 A, zero-cross 0.25 A in auto mode (p7).
- Iq: 7 uA no-load auto mode (13.5 V in, 3.3 V out) (p1); shutdown 0.6 uA typ (p23). FPWM mode available (efficiency 83 % at 1 mA, 13.5 V in, 5 V out, p1).
  TO VERIFY: how FPWM is selected (EN/SYNC clocking vs ordering suffix) - the part-number variants were not read.
- Efficiency 20 V in, 5 V out, 4 A: 95 % at 400 kHz, 93 % at 2.1 MHz (p10).
- 5 V out at 400 kHz: L 8.2 uH, Cout 3 x 22 uF; at 2.1 MHz: L 1.5 uH, Cout 2 x 22 uF (p31, Table 9-2).
- No SPICE model in the datasheet. TI publishes PSpice/TINA models on the product page (check before sim).
- KiCad symbol: none found in the library (generic symbol with same pinout needed). Footprint: Texas_VQFN-HR-* family exists for 12/20 pin only; 14 pin needs a project footprint.
