# TPS65131 (TI, dual boost + inverting, 2.7-5.5 V in) - facts
Source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps65131.pdf (via text proxy)
- VIN 2.7-5.5 V; VPOS up to +15 V, VNEG down to -15 V (p1). Fixed ~1380 kHz (1250-1500) PWM; pulse-skipping at light load (p1, p6) -> not strict forced PWM; at 26 mA + signal load the converter should stay in PWM, check in sim.
- Iq ~500 uA, shutdown 0.2-1.5 uA (p3, p5). Switch current limit TPS65131 1.95 A typ (p5). Vref 1.213 V (p5). Efficiency up to 89 % boost, 81 % inverter (p1).
- +-5.5 V example: L 4.7 uH both stages (p13), Cin 4.7 uF 6.3 V X5R, Cout 4 x 4.7 uF 25 V X7R (p12).
- Package VQFN-24 4x4 (p1); KiCad symbol Regulator_Switching:TPS65131RGE, fp VQFN-24-1EP_4x4mm_P0.5mm_EP2.45x2.45mm_ThermalVias (pins 1-25 incl. EP).
- No SPICE in datasheet (TI product page has PSpice/TINA).
