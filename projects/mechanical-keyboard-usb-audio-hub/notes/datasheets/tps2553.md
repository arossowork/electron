# TPS2553 (TI) SLVS841F, read 2026-10-22 pp1-8 (ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553.pdf)
Adjustable current-limited switch 75 mA - 1.7 A, 85 mohm typ (DBV SOT-23-6), 2.5-6.5 V in, ILIM = external resistor 15-232 k, EN ACTIVE HIGH (TPS2553), FAULT open-drain, reverse input-output voltage protection (comparator trips at VOUT-VIN 95-190 mV, 135 typ, switch off in about 5 ms), constant-current limit (TPS2553; the -1 part latches off).
SOT-23-6 DBV pins (p5): 1 IN | 2 GND | 3 EN | 4 FAULT | 5 ILIM | 6 OUT. (WSON DRV: 1 OUT, 2 ILIM, 3 FAULT, 4 EN, 5 GND, 6 IN, PAD.)
Abs max: all pins -0.3..7 V; IN to OUT -7..7 V; FAULT sink 25 mA; ILIM source 1 mA. Recommended: IN 2.5-6.5 V, EN VIH 1.1 V, VIL 0.66 V; Iout up to 1.5 A at Tj <= 105 C; ILIM 15-232 k; CIN >= 0.1 uF.
Current limit (p7), min/typ/max mA: R 15 k: 1610/1700/1800; R 20 k: 1215/1295/1375; R 49.9 k: 490/520/550; R 210 k: 110/130/150; ILIM shorted to IN: 50/75/100.
Supply: 0.1 uA (1 max) disabled; 100-140 uA enabled. Rdson 85 mohm typ, 95 max at 25 C (135 over temperature). tr 1.1 ms, UVLO 2.35-2.45 V.
Use as the C-port bus-mode limiter: IN = 5V_SYS, OUT = VBUS_C (port side), EN = bus-mode signal.
