Reset 6 mW (1.2 mA), suspend 12.5 mW (2.5 mA), chip disabled (CHIPEN low, QFN76 only) 0.88 mW.
With external 1.2 V and 3.3 V supplies: 4 SS ports = 599 mW at 1.2 V + 80 mW at 3.3 V = 679 mW delivered. At 85-88 % buck efficiency that is ~790 mW from 5 V against 821 mW with the internal regulators: saves ~4 %, not worth the parts.

## Facts for the design
- Features p.9-10: 4/3/2 downstream ports by firmware, per-port enable, BC1.2 CDP/DCP/ACA, individual/gang over-current, built-in 1.5 k upstream pull-up and 15 k downstream pull-downs, SPI-flash or EEPROM firmware/config, SMBus (QFN76 and QFN64) for VID/PID, non-removable ports.
- Pin table p.14-16 (QFN76 column). US port: TXN1_P0/TXP1_P0 68/69, RXN1_P0/RXP1_P0 71/72, DM_P0/DP_P0 67/66. DS1: TX 58/59, RX 61/62, DM/DP 64/63. DS2: TX 20/21, RX 23/24, DM/DP 26/25. DS3: TX 15/16, RX 18/19, DM/DP 14/13. DS4: TX 5/6, RX 8/9, DM/DP 11/10. Always take the final numbers from the project symbol.
- PWREN1~4# pins 37,36,33,32; OVCUR1~4# 38,35,34,31 (active low, internal pull-down). PGANG pin 53 (strap, pull < 100 k down = individual mode). AMBER2 sets power-switch polarity (p.34). PSELF = self/bus power select (p.33). VBUS pin 54 (valid-VBUS detect through divider), RESETJ pin 48 (default pull-up 10 k), CHIPEN 45, X1 57, X2 56, RTERM 46 (20 k to GND, required), SPI CK 50, DO 49, DI 51, CZ 52.
- Supplies: V5 pin 43 and VDDP pin 42 (5 V), V33 pin 44 (3.3 V LDO out, 250 mA max, needs output cap), SW pin 41 and FB pin 39 for the 1.2 V DC-DC (800 mA max; external inductor and FB divider; values are in the Genesys USB3.1 Hub Design Guide, not in this datasheet), VSSP 40. 1.2 V domains: VP12_P0~P4, AVDD12, DVDD12 (pins 70,60,22,17,7 / 4 / 28,47). 3.3 V: DVDD33 55, AVDD33 73/65/12/27.
- Clock p.41: 25 MHz crystal on X1/X2, or external 3.3 V clock on X1 (+-300 ppm, < 50 ps p-p jitter, leave X2 open).
- Config without flash: power-on defaults from ROM; the flash/EEPROM is optional. Not-removable (compound device) ports need flash, SMBus or strapping, otherwise the host sees internal ports as removable (cosmetic).
- Operating supply 4.75-5.25 V, 0-70 C (LCSC page). Absolute maximum ratings are on p.37 (not extracted; read p.37 before the circuit stage uses numbers).
- Hub layout guide (max SS trace length, impedance, skew): NOT in this datasheet. It is in Genesys "USB3.1 Hub Design Guide" (needs the Genesys FAE). Open item for architecture section 6 (165 mm upstream).

## Iteration 2: package and symbol (read as images, Rev 1.10)
- QFN76 package drawing p.42 (Figure 7.1): body 9.00 x 9.00, pitch e = 0.40, lead width b 0.15-0.25 (0.20 nom), L 0.30-0.50, thickness A 0.70-0.90, EP D2 x E2 = 5.10-6.40, nominal 5.75 x 5.75 (square). Pin 1 at the bottom-left of the bottom edge (top view), pins 1-19 bottom (left to right), 20-38 right (bottom to top), 39-57 top (right to left), 58-76 left (top to bottom), EP = GND.
- Footprint: the library QFN-76-1EP_9x9mm_P0.4mm_EP5.81x6.31mm covers it (EP inside 5.10-6.40; the 3.8 x 3.8 variant is too small). KiCad numbers pad 1 at the top of the left edge, counter-clockwise: the same cyclic order as Genesys, rotated by 90 degrees, so the placer may use any rotation; no project footprint needed. Check the JLC pin-1 rotation at the CPL stage.
- Full QFN76 pin table p.14-16, project symbol kb_hub:GL3523 (77 pins: 76 + EP number 77).
