# HD3SS3220 (TI) USB Type-C DRP port controller with 2:1 SuperSpeed mux, RNH WQFN-30 2.5 x 4.5 mm

Source: SLLSES1E (Dec 2015, rev Jul 2025), https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/hd3ss3220.pdf (read as page images; text extraction is refused in this session). LCSC C701817 (HD3SS3220IRNHR, -40..85 C).

## Pins (p.3-4, Figure 4-1, top view, pin 1 top left, counter-clockwise)
1 CC2, 2 CC1, 3 CURRENT_MODE (tri-level, DFP only, dont care in UFP), 4 PORT (L = UFP, NC = DRP, H = DFP), 5 VBUS_DET (5-28 V through 900 k external resistor),
6 TXp, 7 TXn, 8 VCC33, 9 RXp, 10 RXn (host side of the mux), 11 DIR (open drain, orientation; needs 200 k pull-up), 12 ENn_MUX (L = on),
13 GND, 14 RX1n, 15 RX1p, 16 TX1n, 17 TX1p, 18 RX2n, 19 RX2p, 20 TX2n, 21 TX2p (connector side), 22 ADDR (H = I2C 0x67, L = I2C 0x47, NC = GPIO mode),
23 INT_N/OUT3, 24 VCONN_FAULT_N (open drain), 25 SDA/OUT1, 26 SCL/OUT2, 27 ID (open drain, low when a device is attached as DFP), 28 GND, 29 ENn_CC (L = enabled), 30 VDD5, thermal pad (31 in the project symbol) = GND.

## Host Rp current level in UFP mode (answers the pin question)
- GPIO mode (ADDR left NC, PORT tied to GND): SDA/OUT1 (pin 25) and SCL/OUT2 (pin 26) are open-drain outputs (p.4):
  OUT1: H = Default (900 mA), L = Medium (1.5 A) or High (3 A).  OUT2: H = Default or Medium, L = High (3 A).
  Decode: OUT1 H, OUT2 H = default; OUT1 L, OUT2 H = 1.5 A; OUT1 L, OUT2 L = 3 A. External pull-ups 200 k (Rp_ODext, p.5) to 3.3 V.
- I2C mode (ADDR high/low, 0x67 / 0x47): Connection Status register 0x08, CURRENT_MODE_DETECT bits 5:4: 00 default, 01 medium, 10 charge-through accessory 500 mA, 11 high. SDA/SCL 1.65-3.6 V.
- INT_N/OUT3 in GPIO mode = audio accessory detect (L = detected). Not used.

## Ratings (p.5)
Absolute max: VDD5 -0.3..6 V, VCC33 -0.3..4 V, control pins -0.3..VDD5+0.3 (ENn_MUX and DIR: VCC33+0.3), CC1/CC2 -0.3..6 V, VBUS_DET -0.3..4 V (through 900 k), SS pins -0.3..2.5 V. ESD HBM 2 kV, CDM 1.5 kV.
Recommended: VDD5 4.5-5.5 V, VCC33 3-3.6 V, bulk C 10-200 uF on VDD5 only when VCONN is on (p.5), pull-ups on open-drain pins 200 k, on PORT/ADDR 4.7 k.
CC1/CC2 absolute max is 6 V: the CC lines can see 20 V only if a cable fault occurs; add a CC TVS/protection chosen by the power group.
CIO and Ron of the SS mux were not read (not in the extract); circuit stage: read p.6-7 table if the SS budget needs them.

## Package RNH0030A (p.45-46)
Body 2.5 x 4.5, 0.8 max, pitch 0.4, land pads 0.5 x 0.2 (sides at x = +-1.2, ends at y = +-2.2, end pads 0.2 x 0.5), EP 1.2 x 3.2 (outline p.45, example land pattern p.46). Project footprint kb_hub:WQFN-30-1EP_2.5x4.5mm_P0.4mm_EP1.2x3.2mm.
Deviation: corner pads 1, 10, 16, 25 are 0.4 mm long (TI draws a chamfer) so that the diagonal gap to the end pads is 0.15 mm.
