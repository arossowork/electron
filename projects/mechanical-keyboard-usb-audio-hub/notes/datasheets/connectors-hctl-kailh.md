# Connectors and hot-swap socket: what could and could not be read (iteration 2)

## HCTL HC-TYPE-C-24P-MM-1.4-5A-12 (LCSC C5307768), USB-C 24P mid-mount, sink 1.4
- LCSC page: "surface mount, right-angle", 24-pos, -40..85 C. The datasheet link is https://www.lcsc.com/datasheet/C5307768.pdf (viewer page only: not retrievable as a file, 2 attempts; guessed wmsc/atta URLs failed, EasyEDA has no footprint for it: API says "Component not found").
- Sister drawings that WERE readable (other variants of the same HCTL 24P family, not this part): HC-TYPE-C-24P-VSX-5A-F1.1-04 (vertical, 8.94 wide x 7.00, L 8.8/9.3/10.5) and HC-TYPE-C-24P-DSX-CH2.75S-5A-04 (front-through, rear-SMT, raised 2.75, height 3.3, 9.0 wide), https://atta.szlcsc.com/upload/public/pdf/source/20230112/B09D28C3B91A95668159AA2DF70E272A.pdf. Pin table equals the USB-C standard (A1 GND, A2 SSTXP1, A3 SSTXN1, A4 VBUS, A5 CC1, A6 Dp1, A7 Dn1, A8 SBU1, A9 VBUS, A10 SSRXN2, A11 SSRXP2, A12 GND, B-row mirrored).
- Standard Type-C receptacle shell: 8.94 mm wide x 3.26 mm tall (drawing of the sister parts: 8.94 wide, 3.3 high shell). Mid-mount: the body sits in a board cut-out, so the part above the board top is below 3.3 mm: it fits the 11.1 mm stack with margin.
- Footprint kb_hub:USB_C_Receptacle_HCTL_HC-TYPE-C-24P-MM-1.4 is PROVISIONAL (copy of the JAE DX07S024WJ1R350 mid-mount pad geometry in the KiCad library: A row y=-2.3, B row y=+2.3, pitch 0.5, pads 0.27 x 1.4, six shield slots). Not from the HCTL drawing. Must be compared with the HCTL drawing (open the LCSC datasheet in a browser) before any board release.

## HCTL HC-ST-003-01-Z (LCSC C2845331), USB 3.0 type A right angle THT, 9P
- LCSC: USB 3.0 type A female, right angle, through hole, 9 contacts, 30 V, -25..70 C. Datasheet viewer URL https://www.lcsc.com/datasheet/C2845331.pdf (not retrievable as a file, 2 attempts). No height on the page.
- Pad geometry from the LCSC/EasyEDA footprint of this exact part ("USB-3.0-A-TH_HC-ST-003-01-Z", easyeda.com/api/products/C2845331/components): pads 1-4 at x = 0, 2.5, 4.5, 7.0 (y = 0), pads 5-9 at x = 7.5, 5.5, 3.5, 1.5, -0.5 (y = -1.5), hole 0.8, pad 1.2; shield: two pads 3.0 with 2.3 mm hole at (-3.05, 2.10) and (10.05, 2.10). Same signal pad pattern as Molex 48393-001.
- Height: standard USB 3.0 type A right-angle shell is ~6.6-7.2 mm above the board top (class value, not from the HCTL drawing). With 1.6 mm board: 8.2-8.8 mm overall, inside the 11.1 mm limit (the floorplan assumes the tallest front part at 7.5 mm above the top: marginal against 7.2; confirm with the vendor drawing).
- Body depth (front face at y = 12.3 from pin 1) is an ESTIMATE from the Molex footprint.

## Kailh CPG151101S11 hot-swap socket (C2803348 / -1 C5184526 / -16 C5156480)
- Kailh datasheet http://www.kailh.com/product/Ms/rcb/CPG151101S11-16.pdf: unreachable (expired certificate). SMD size 14.5 x 5.9 mm per the distributor listing.
- Geometry used: switch pins at (-3.81, -2.54) and (2.54, -5.08) (MX standard), pads 2.55 x 2.5 at 3.275 mm outward of each pin (ai03 / keebio community footprints, widely built), 4.0 mm centre hole, 1.7 mm plate-pin holes at +-5.08, 3.0 mm holes at the two pin positions (PM: "3 mm locating holes"). Cross-check: the LCSC EasyEDA footprint of C5156480 (SMD_KEYBOARD-SW) has pads 2.5 x 2.55 mm at 3.55 mm outward (0.25 mm wider) and 1.5 mm pin holes: the Kailh drawing decides; verify before layout release.
- EasyEDA footprint of C2803348 is a through-hole variant ("TH_L10.9-W5.9-CPG151101S11") and is not a reference.

## Iteration 3 (2026-10-22): footprint verification attempts
- HCTL C5307768 (USB-C MM-1.4) and C2845331 (USB-A): LCSC datasheet URL is a viewer (WebFetch returns no file); guessed wmsc/datasheet.lcsc.com URLs return nothing or redirect to the viewer. Drawings NOT obtained (more than 2 attempts spent). The SHOU HAN TYPE-C 24P QCHT drawing (C456013, readable) is a front-through / rear-SMT part for 1.0 or 1.2 mm boards: not usable with the 1.6 mm board. JAE DX07S024WJ1R350 (the KiCad footprint source) is out of stock at all distributors (Mouser 1 pc 3.70 USD, 15 weeks lead): not a replacement.
- USB-C footprint therefore stays PROVISIONAL (JAE geometry). Required before release: open https://www.lcsc.com/datasheet/C5307768.pdf in a browser (or ask HCTL) and compare pads (A row y=-2.3, B row y=+2.3, pitch 0.5, 0.27 x 1.4), the shell tabs and the board cut-out (not modelled in the footprint: a mid-mount needs a board cut-out of about 9 x 4 mm under the body, to be added as a floorplan cut region by the layout stage once the drawing gives the size).
- USB-A: signal pads 1-9 are identical to the KiCad Molex 48393-001 footprint (checked with hwflow lib pads: x 0/2.5/4.5/7.0 at y 0 and 7.5/5.5/3.5/1.5/-0.5 at y -1.5). Shield holes of the project footprint (round 3.0 / 2.3 drill at (-3.05, 2.1), (10.05, 2.1)) come from the EasyEDA footprint of the exact part; the Molex footprint uses slots at x -2.9 / 9.9: do not change without the HCTL drawing. Height class from a readable sister drawing (Wurth WR-COM 692122030100 USB 3.0 A horizontal, we-online datasheet p.1: body 7.0 mm, 7.55 mm max above the board seating plane, 16.66 mm long, 13.1 wide shell): expect 6.5-7.55 mm. Floorplan allows 7.5 mm: the HCTL part must be measured (risk).
- Kailh CPG151101S11: Kailh PDF unreachable. Footprint corrected to the kiswitch library SW_Hotswap_Kailh_MX_1.00u (github.com/kiswitch/kiswitch, library/footprints/Switch_Keyboard_Hotswap_Kailh.pretty): pad 1 at (-7.085, -2.54), pad 2 at (5.842, -5.08) (front view, B.Cu), size 2.55 x 2.5; holes 3.05 at (-3.81, -2.54) and (2.54, -5.08), 4.0 centre, 1.75 plate pins at +-5.08. Changes from iteration 2: pad 2 x 5.815 -> 5.842, hole 3.0 -> 3.05, 1.7 -> 1.75. Still a community footprint, not the Kailh drawing.
- Encoder EC11E153440D (C370992): LCSC/search: 11 mm Alps EC11E, 15 pulses / 30 detents, push switch, vertical, 5 V 10 mA. Alps PDF returned 403 / timeout, so no drawing read. KiCad Rotary_Encoder:RotaryEncoder_Alps_EC11E-Switch_Vertical_H20mm (Alps-based): pads A (0,0), C (0,2.5), B (0,5) 2.0 mm; switch S2 (14.5,0), S1 (14.5,5); mount tabs MP at (7.5,-3.1) and (7.5,8.1) 3.2 x 2.0. The H20mm is the shaft length (20 mm); the pad grid is the same for all EC11E vertical push versions, so the footprint is kept; the shaft length of the "40D" variant is unverified and does not change the pads.
- Scratch check `hwflow check` (project libraries via `libraries: [<abs path>/lib]` in a design.yaml inside the project scratch folder, since /tmp is not writable): GL3523, HD3SS3220, HD3SS3212, USB-C (24 pins + S1), USB-A (10 pins incl. shield), Kailh socket, 2 stabilizer footprints, encoder all load; every symbol pin maps to a footprint pad (no unknown/unmapped pin or pad error; a deliberate wrong pin number was reported as unknown-pin + unmapped-pin, so the check is live). Only noise remained: single-pin nets and power-source warnings of the artificial nets, and "no pin-to-net map" for pad-less stabilizer parts H1, H2 (check requires `pins:`; `pins: {}` was not enough, the circuit stage must give them a pin map or `nc`).
