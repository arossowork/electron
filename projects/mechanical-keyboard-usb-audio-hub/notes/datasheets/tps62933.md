# TPS62933F (TI, 3 A, 3.8-30 V buck, FCCM) - facts
Source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62933.pdf (via text proxy; first 100k chars)
- Variants (p3): TPS62932 2 A PFM; TPS62933 3 A PFM; TPS62933F 3 A FCCM (fixed frequency at light load, p26); TPS62933P PG; TPS62933O out-of-audio.
- VIN 3.8-30 V, abs max 32 V (p1, p5). VOUT 0.8-22 V, Vref 0.8 V +-1 % (p1). SOT583-8 1.6x2.1 mm. Pins: RT1 EN2 VIN3 GND4 SW5 BST6 SS/PG7 FB8 (p3).
- Iq typ: 12 uA (PFM versions), 125 uA (F version), 45 uA (O version) (p6).
- Rds_on 76 mohm high / 32 mohm low (25 C) (p6). Current limit high 4.2-5.8 A, low 2.9-4.5 A (p6-7).
- fsw: RT floating 500 kHz, RT to GND 1.2 MHz, resistor 200-2200 kHz (p19-20).
- Efficiency VIN 24 V, 5 V out, 500 kHz, L 6.8 uH: ~95 % at 3 A, ~85 % at 0.1 A (F version, p11 Fig 8-22).
- 5 V out, 500 kHz: L 6.8 uH, Cout 20 uF; 3.3 V out, 1.2 MHz: L 2.2 uH, Cout 30 uF (p31).
- No SPICE model in the datasheet (TI product page has PSpice/TINA; check).
