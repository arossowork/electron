## Board integration (p26-29)
- >= 8 x 100 nF low-inductance MLCC on VDD close to the chip; one 100 nF 0402 per VDDIO pin; bulk >= 10 uF on VDD and each VDDIO.
- PLL_AVDD (0.9 V): 1 uF MLCC + ferrite 600 ohm @100 MHz DCR < 1 ohm (Taiyo Yuden BKH1005LM601-T); separate from noisy supplies.
- USB_VDD18 (pin 31, 1.8 V analog) and USB_VDD33 (pin 30, 3.3 V analog) can be left open if USB is unused.
- Supplies monotonic; VDDIO and VDD within 50 ms of each other; VDDIOL/R/T valid before the device leaves reset (boot pins on VDDIOL). If VDDIOB18 is off while VDD is on, the 3.3 V IO rails default to 1.8 V mode: with 3.3 V VDDIO the 3.3 V must not exceed 1.98 V while VDDIOB18 is off unless VDD is off; do not turn VDDIOB18 on last. External RST_N may be held low until supplies are valid (Fig 27b).
- 16 vias (4x4) under the ground paddle; a VDD via under each VDD paddle. MSL 3. USB 90 ohm diff: W 0.12 mm, S1 0.10 mm, H 0.10 mm, S2 0.51, S3 1.27, skew 1 mm.

## Reset, boot, clock (p14-19, p78-79)
- Crystal: 24 MHz +-500 ppm or better, XIN/XOUT on VDDIOB18 (1.8 V); examples Fig 11 (p15): CL 12 pF, max ESR 30-60 ohm, Rd 680 ohm in series with XOUT, Cl1 = Cl2 = 22 pF (Seiko FA-238, Multicomp MCSJK-7U, IQD LFXTAL032813, TKC 7M-24.000MAAE-T). An external clock on XIN must be 1.8 V, low jitter, monotonic edges; 12 or 24 MHz when USB is used.
- Reset: on-chip POR holds reset until VDD and VDDIOB18 are valid; RST_N is "NC, or external reset" (1.8 V domain, internal pull-up; do not drive above 1.8 V + 0.5 V). With 3.3 V VDDIOx an external RST_N must stay low until all VDDIO are valid, or VDDIOL/R/T must be valid before VDD and VDDIOB18 are (checklist G.3). Boot starts T(INIT) 290-480 us after reset release.
- Boot source pins X0D04/X0D05/X0D06 (internal weak pull-down during reset): 000 = QSPI flash, 001 = SPI flash, 010 SPI slave. QSPI boot (p18): pins X0D01 CS, X0D04 IO0, X0D05 IO1, X0D06 IO2, X0D07 IO3, X0D10 SCLK; Quad I/O read 0xEB, 3 address bytes + 1 dummy; flash must be ready within 300 us after power-up (else hold RST_N low) and be in a power-up state where quad-mode reads work (QE bit set: choose W25Q64JVSSIM, which has QE = 1 from the factory, or program QE once). X0D01 CS needs an external pull-up to VDDIOL (Fig 2 uses a resistor to VDDIOL).
- Checklist G/H (p78-80): PLL_AVDD low-pass filtered; bulk >= 10 uF each supply; flash supported by the XMOS tools (xflash); xSYS2 0.05" JTAG header or another way to program flash/OTP; X0D04-X0D07 are output only during/after reset; decoupling spread around the device, ground path from each cap to the centre pad; PLL_AVDD cap close to the pin.

## Design decisions in this project (PM round 6)
- Rails: VDD + PLL_AVDD 0.9 V from a TPS62933F buck (5V_SYS in, 500 kHz, 4.7 uH, FB 10.0 k / 80.6 k = 0.8993 V, 3 x 22 uF); VDDIOB18 + USB_VDD18 1.8 V from AP2112K-1.8 LDO off 3V3_D; VDDIOL/R/T + USB_VDD33 3.3 V from 3V3_D. Order: 3V3_D, then 1.8 V, then 0.9 V (0.9 V buck EN = 1.8 V rail: VDD never on while VDDIOB18 is off, satisfies p26). RST_N not driven (internal POR).
