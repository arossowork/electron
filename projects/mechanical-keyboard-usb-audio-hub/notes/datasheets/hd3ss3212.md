# HD3SS3212 (TI) 2-channel 2:1 USB 3.1 Gen 1 mux, RKS VQFN-20 2.5 x 4.5 mm

Source: SLASE74F (Sep 2016), https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/hd3ss3212.pdf (read as page images). LCSC C129320 (HD3SS3212RKSR).

## Pins (p.4)
1 RSVD1, 2 OEn (L = normal, H = shutdown), 3 A0p, 4 A0n, 5 GND, 6 VCC (3.3 V), 7 A1p, 8 A1n, 9 SEL (L = A to B, H = A to C), 10 RSVD2, 11 GND,
12 C1n, 13 C1p, 14 C0n, 15 C0p, 16 B1n, 17 B1p, 18 B0n, 19 B0p, 20 GND, thermal pad = GND (pin 21 in the project symbol).
RSVD1/RSVD2: not connected. Unselected ports have 20 k pull-downs switched in. Channels: 0 and 1 are the two differential pairs (TX and RX).

## Package RKS0020A (p.20-21)
Body 2.5 x 4.5, 1.0 max, pitch 0.5. Side pads 0.6 x 0.25 at x = +-1.15 (pins 2-9 left, 12-19 right, 19 at the top), y = +-1.75 max; end pads 0.25 x 0.6 at y = +-2.15 (1, 20 at the top, x = -0.25, +0.25; 10, 11 at the bottom); EP 1.0 x 3.0.
Project footprint kb_hub:VQFN-20-1EP_2.5x4.5mm_P0.5mm_EP1.0x3.0mm. Insertion loss -1.6 dB at 5 GHz, Ron 5 ohm, BW 8 GHz: from the earlier pass (p.1/p.6, not re-read).
