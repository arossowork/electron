# CH224K / IP2736U / CH236D - facts and gaps
- CH224K (WCH, PD3.0 sink, ESSOP10, LCSC C970725): 4-22 V supply, up to 20 V 5 A, BC1.2, PG pin for PMOS gate, CFG1 (or CFG1-3) sets voltage, E-mark emulation, VCONN detect (components101.com/node/2294, flux.ai). Stock 0 / 11 724 depending on the source and date, price $0.28 at 100 (LCSC listing). Pin table, CFG1 20 V level, Iq NOT read (PDF behind captcha: uelectronics mirror; WCH CH224DS1.PDF). No KiCad symbol in the library (CH236D and CH246D exist, CH224 does not). TO DO before circuit stage: fetch WCH CH224DS1.PDF in a browser.
- IP2736U_EF (Injoinic, QFN-24 4x4, LCSC C42437970): single-port PD 2.0/3.1/PPS/EPR source protocol IC, 3-25 V VIN, abs max 30 V; it does NOT contain a buck: it drives an external buck/AC-DC through PWM feedback, optocoupler or I2C, has an NMOS driver and low-side current sense (lcsc datasheet C42437970, p1, p4-8, p11). Stock 4 526 at $0.56 (1 k). No KiCad symbol (IP2721 only).
- CH236D (WCH, QFN-20 3x3, in KiCad as Interface_USB:CH236D): PD2.0/3.0, PPS, BC1.2 source controller. Pins from lib: GND, FBOX, VHV, GATEP, GATEN, ~NMOS, VDD, UD+, UD-, CC1, CC2, IS+, IS-, VFB, IFB, VBUS, FBO. Function guess: FB-injection onto an external buck (FBO/VFB), VBUS gate drivers GATEP/GATEN, shunt IS+/IS-. Datasheet not read (WCH CH236DS1.PDF; page lists it but content is not text-extractable here). LCSC number and stock not verified.
