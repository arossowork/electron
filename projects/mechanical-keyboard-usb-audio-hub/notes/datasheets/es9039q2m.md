## Package and pins (p.10-12, p.104)
32-pin QFN (WQFN, MO-220) 5.0 x 5.0 x 0.75 mm, pitch 0.50, pad width 0.25 (0.18-0.30), L 0.40 (0.35-0.45), EP 3.20 x 3.20 (3.15-3.25), pin 33 = EP -> GND. Pin 1 top-left of the left side, counter-clockwise (1-8 left, 9-16 bottom, 17-24 right, 25-32 top).
1 AVCC_DAC1 | 2 DAC1B | 3 DAC1 | 4 AGND_DAC1 | 5 AGND_DAC2 | 6 DAC2B | 7 DAC2 | 8 AVCC_DAC2 | 9 DVDD (1.2 V internal, 1 uF tantalum) | 10 AVDD (3.3 V digital reg supply) | 11 GND | 12 DATA_CLK (I2S BCLK) | 13 DATA1 (I2S WS) | 14 DATA2 (I2S DATA) | 15 SCLK/SCL/HW1 | 16 MOSI/SDA/HW0 | 17 RT1 (tie to GND) | 18 CHIP_EN (active high) | 19 SS/ADDR1/HW2 | 20 MISO/ADDR0/MUTE_CTRL | 21 GPIO1 (automute out, reset Ground) | 22 GPIO2 (lock status out) | 23 GPIO3 | 24 GPIO4 | 25 GPIO5 | 26 GPIO6 | 27 GPIO7 | 28 GPIO8 (cal resistor) | 29 MODE | 30 GND | 31 VCCA (3.3 V analog) | 32 MCLK | 33 EP.
Modes (p.13): MODE=GND I2C; MODE=AVDD SPI; pull-low/pull-high = hardware mode. I2C address (ADDR1/ADDR0 to GND/AVDD): 0x90, 0x92, 0x94, 0x96. PCM default pins: DATA_CLK=BCLK, DATA1=WS, DATA2=DATA; **master or slave** (p.28).

## Supplies and absolute maximum (p.50-51)
AVCC_DAC1/2 3.3 V +-15 % (abs max -0.3..+3.8 V); AVDD 3.3 V +-5 % (max 3.7 V); VCCA 3.3 V +-5 % (max 3.7 V); DVDD 1.2 V internal (max 1.4 V). Digital input -0.3 V to AVDD+0.3 V; VIH (AVDD/2)+0.4, VIL 0.4, VOH AVDD-0.2, VOL 0.2. HBM 2 kV, CDM 500 V. Tj max 125 C, Ta -20..+85 C.
Power-up (p.51): AVDD, then ~200 us later VCCA, then AVCC_DAC1/2, then CHIP_EN. Power-down reverse.
"All AVCC_DAC1/AVCC_DAC2 pins must be powered with an ultra-low-noise regulator" (p.97; ESS ES9312Q is the intended part). Max MCLK 50 MHz (p.54).

## Power consumption (p.52-53), slave/master, 3.3 V supplies, fs = 48 kHz
Full-scale 1 kHz: master, MCLK 24.576 MHz (FS = MCLK/512): AVCC_DAC1 6.8 + AVCC_DAC2 6.8 + VCCA 0.5 + AVDD 9.4 mA = 23.5 mA (78 mW). Slave, MCLK 49.152: 5.9+5.9+0.4+4.0 = 16.2 mA (54 mW). Master, 12.288 MHz: 19 mA (64 mW).
Silence (automute): master 24.576: 1.5+1.5+0.44+6.9 = 10.3 mA (34 mW); slave 49.152: 4.4 mA (15 mW).
(No 96/192 kHz current is given: budget +25 % for 192 kHz.)

## Performance (p.54), eval board v1.0, I2S master, fs 48 kHz, 10 Vrms = 0 dBFS
THD+N (diff, 0 dBFS): -120 dB at 48 kHz (BW 20-20 kHz), -117 dB at 96 kHz, -114 dB at 192 kHz; THD -126 dB. DNR (A-wtd, -60 dBFS): **130 dB** stereo, 133 dB mono. Resolution 32 bit.
**Analog output**: full-scale voltage 0.889 x AVCC Vpp per pin (2.93 Vpp at 3.3 V), bias AVCC/2; full-scale current 1000 x 0.889 x AVCC / Rdac mApp (7.5 mApp per pin at 3.3 V); offset current 1000 x (AVCC/2 - Vg)/Rdac mA (4.2 mA if the I/V virtual ground Vg = 0); **Rdac = 390 ohm +-15 %** per output pin. Calibration of the level against Rdac spread: internal ~50 kohm resistor on GPIO8 (p.48) with an ES9312Q regulator; without it the level is +-15 % (use the digital volume).

## Recommended I/V (p.98-99, EVB v2.1)
Dual op-amp stage (OPA1612): per side Rf = 3.6k || 3.6k = 1.8 kohm with 510 pF C0G across it (gain Rf/Rdac = 4.6 -> 10 Vrms diff at 0 dBFS, rails +-15 V), then 140 ohm + 2.2 nF at the output, 4.7k thin-film bleeders. Triple stage adds a 3rd OPA1612 diff-to-SE (4.3k / 10k, 51 pF / 1.2 M). Thin-film resistors and C0G capacitors recommended. For 2 Vrms diff FS: Rf = 2.83 Vpp / 7.52 mApp = 376 ohm (374 ohm E96), gain 0.96.
Power supply (p.100): ES9312Q (needs 5 V) feeding AVCC_DAC1/2 with 4.7 uF tantalum + 100 nF C0G each; 1 uF caps on AVCC_DAC and DVDD, 1 uF tantalum on DVDD and AVCC (p.97 note 2). Decoupling: C6/C7 1 uF on AVDD/VCCA, 1 uF C10 on MCLK/VCCA.
Layout (p.101): 4-layer, unbroken ground plane under the chip, short bypass paths, EP to the ground plane with several vias.

## Notes for the design
- Noise at the project gain: DNR 130 dB is re 10 Vrms through the 1.8 k eval stage (gain 4.6, op-amp noise included). Inverting the numbers: eval floor 3.16 uV, op-amp part ~1.3 uV, DAC part ~2.9 uV at gain 4.6; at gain 0.96 the DAC part is 0.60 uV (derived [E]).
- No separate 1.8 V rail; I2C slave interface; DAC can be I2S master from its own MCLK (HW mode 2: MCLK 24.576 MHz / 512 = 48 kHz).
