# IP2736U (Injoinic), read 2026-10-22
Source: datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/fa546fa55e17fb06f11f04b1719b76c3.pdf (V1.01, 20 pages). LCSC C42437970 (IP2736U_EF), QFN-24 4x4 0.5 mm, 3944 in stock, $0.5634 at 100.

Features (p1): Type-C source (DFP), PD2.0/3.1/PPS/EPR 28 V, PD USB-IF TID 10162, e-marker support, QC/FCP/AFC and others, BC1.2. Programmable voltage loop (min step 10 mV) and current loop, low-side current sense, cable-loss compensation. Voltage adjust by PWM controller feedback (FB pin acts on the buck feedback node), optocoupler drive, or I2C. NMOS driver for the VBUS path, NMOS Vds sense, AUTOMATIC BLEED (VBUS discharge), standby low-power mode. OCP/OVP/SCP, up to 4 NTC inputs. DP/DM/CC1/CC2 20 V tolerant. Customizable and field upgradable: the PDO list is a variant/firmware property NOT shown in the datasheet.

Pins (p9-10): 1 VCC (internal LDO out, 2.2 uF) | 2 CMPV (loop V compensation) | 3 CMPI/SDA | 4 SCL (or GPIO3) | 5 FB (drive output to the buck feedback) | 6 GPIO1 (ADC) | 7 GPIO0 (ADC) | 8, 9 NC | 10 CSN | 11 CSP | 12 GPIO11 | 13 GPIO10 | 14 CC2 | 15 DP | 16 DM | 17 CC1 | 18 GND | 19 NTC | 20 VOUT2 | 21 VOUT2G | 22 VOUT1 | 23 VOUT1G | 24 VIN | 25 EPAD (GND).

Abs max (p11): VIN, VOUT, VOUTG, DP/DM, CC1/CC2, FB: -0.3..30 V; other pins -0.3..6 V; Tj 150 C; HBM 4 kV; theta JA 90 C/W. Recommended VIN 3-25 V. UVLO 2.5-2.9 V. VCC 3.2 V. Rp current sources (p12): 80 uA default, 180 uA 1.5 A, 330 uA 3.0 A. GPIO Rpu 3k, Rpd 20k, VIH 0.7 VCC. I2C 100-400 kHz.

Typical circuit Fig. 1 (p4), "control PWM controller feedback": VIN through 28 ohm from the converter output; N-FET (Vds >= 30 V) in the VBUS path, gate on VOUT1G (23), VOUT1 (22) through 1 k to the port-side VBUS; CSP/CSN across a 10 mohm shunt; R1 100 k (1 %) from the converter output to its FB node, R2 sets the default 5 V; FB (5) through 100 k and 3.3 nF with CMPV/CMPI RC (1 k, 200 k, 3.3 nF) compensation; 330 pF on CC1/CC2; NTC 10 k (B 3380); VCC 2.2 uF. Quiescent current not given in the pages read.

Package (p13-15): QFN24 4.0 x 4.0, pitch 0.5, lead 0.25 x 0.4, EP 2.5 x 2.5 (land 3.1 x 3.1, 9 vias 0.3), board pad 0.28 x 0.85, overall land 4.8 x 4.8, stencil EP 4 windows 1.0 x 1.0.

Unknown (risk): factory PDO list and current limits of the _EF variant; behaviour of the CC pins when VIN = 0 (assumed high-Z); no SPICE model.
