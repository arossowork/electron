# ADS1115 (TI) 16-bit I2C ADC

Source: ti.com/product/ADS1115 (fetched 2026-10-20). The product page does not give INL, offset or power-down current: stage 3 must read them from the datasheet (values used in architecture.md are marked "assumed").

- Supply 2.0-5.5 V; 150 uA in continuous conversion
- 16-bit, 8-860 SPS, internal reference, PGA +-0.256 V to +-6.144 V
- 4 single-ended or 2 differential inputs; I2C, 4 addresses
- Packages: X2QFN 2 x 1.5 mm, SOT-5X3 2.9 x 2.8 mm, VSSOP-10 3 x 4.9 mm

## Stage 3 additions (2026-10-20)
- LCSC C37593 ADS1115IDGSR, VSSOP-10 3x3 mm (KiCad Package_SO:VSSOP-10_3x3mm_P0.5mm), 4227 in stock, $1.07 at 10.
- Electrical (TI SBAS444E s5.5, from a search snippet, page not verified): at FSR +-2.048 V offset +-3 LSB single-ended / +-1 LSB differential; gain error 0.01 % typ, 0.15 % max at 25 C; INL 1 LSB at 8 SPS; operating 150 uA typ.
- From memory (verify): power-down 0.5 uA typ / 2 uA max; abs max analog input GND-0.3 to VDD+0.3 V, 10 mA continuous, 100 mA momentary; common-mode input impedance about 6 MOhm at FSR +-4.096 V, differential 15 MOhm. ESD HBM 2 kV.
- TI HTML datasheet viewer returns only the table of contents through WebFetch; PDF text extraction was blocked. No SPICE model found; use a fit.
- ADS1113/ADS1114 have a single differential input pair (the KiCad ADS1113IDGS symbol hides pins 2, 6, 7): cannot read Ve, N1, N2 without a mux.
