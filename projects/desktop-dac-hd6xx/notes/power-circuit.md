# Rev A power circuit (component-level candidate)

`design.yaml` contains the integrated circuit, not a released board. Its power-section components expose `V5_SYS`, `VPOS`, `VNEG`, `3V3_D`, `3V3_C`, `3V3_A`, `DAC_EN`, and `PWR_GOOD`. The external adapter must supply regulated, center-positive 5 V; there is no mains on the board. The USB receiver is separately USB-powered with common `GND`. The analog and USB/DAC fragments own `AMP_ENABLE` and DAC mute, respectively.

## Power paths

J300 (CUI PJ-102AH) enters F300 (1.5 A hold PTC), M300 (AO3401A reverse-polarity P-MOS), and `V5_SYS`. D300 is a transient suppressor. M300's drain is on the fused input, source on `V5_SYS`, and gate pulled low. The [AO3401A datasheet](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO3401A.pdf) gives at most 60 mΩ at −4.5 V gate drive; the relay's [237 Ω ±10% coil](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g6k.pdf) draws 21.1 mA nominal and 23.4 mA at its low-resistance limit at 5 V. Budget at least 35 mA on `V5_SYS` for that relay. The TVS does not regulate sustained overvoltage, and its transient clamp exceeds the 6 V absolute maximum of some downstream parts. Qualify the adapter, inrush, and fault survival.

U310 [TPS65131](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps65131.pdf) converts `V5_SYS` to about +10.5 V/−10 V raw with 4.7 µH inductors, Schottky diodes, TI example feedback values (1 MΩ/130 kΩ positive; 1 MΩ/121 kΩ negative), and TI's 6.8 pF/7.5 pF feed-forward capacitors. `PSP` and `PSN` low select forced PWM. Output capacitance is 2 × 10 µF, 25 V, per raw rail; exact MPN and effective capacitance under bias remain to be selected. `BSW` is left open per TI's option without a battery-disconnect switch, so the positive raw output is not fully disconnected in converter shutdown.

**QFN pad escape.** U310 is available in TI's 0.5 mm-pitch RGE VQFN package. Its ~0.25 mm-wide pads and this board's 0.20 mm clearance leave at most `2 × (0.5 − 0.125 − 0.20) = 0.35 mm` for a straight escape between unlike pad nets. Set `board.power_track_mm: 0.30` in the integrated design, keeping `V5_SYS.current_a: 1.5` and each raw rail's `current_a: 0.15` unchanged: the latter route at 0.30 mm, while the 1.5 A main bus still calculates to 0.6 mm and still needs a local neck at its U310 pad. The compiler allows up to 3 mm per local neck and warns on track-current shortfall, but the DSL cannot specify an exact per-pad neck length/shape. [TI's layout guidance](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps65131.pdf) requires wide, short main current paths and close input/output capacitors, inductors, and diodes; verify the actual routed neck, capacitor loops, peak/ripple current, and copper temperature before release. A 0.30 mm global power-class minimum does not waive any net's stated `current_a` or a larger explicit minimum.

U311 [TPS7A3901](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a39.pdf) post-regulates to approximately +9.09 V and −8.93 V: `1.188 × (1 + 66.5/10)` and `−1.19 × 75/10`. Nominal headroom is ~1.4 V/+rail and ~1.1 V/−rail. Crucially, **U311 EN is tied to its positive input `P10V5_RAW`**, not 5 V: TI requires EN not to exceed VINP and recommends connecting EN to VINP when no separate control is used. Each regulated output is rated 150 mA. The two 4.7 µH inductors are Sunlord MWSA0503S-4R7MT, matching the footprint and the [manufacturer's series data](https://www.sunlordinc.com/uploads/files/20230303/MWSA-S%C2%A0series%C2%A0of%C2%A0SMD%C2%A0Power%C2%A0Inductor.pdf). Full analog load, heat, and switching-noise margin are still unmeasured.

## DAC start and ordered adapter-loss shutdown

Three fixed, low-noise [TPS7A2033PDBVR](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a20.pdf) regulators feed ESS AVDD (`3V3_D`), VCCA (`3V3_C`), and AVCC_DAC1/2 (`3V3_A`). All three take input from `V5_HOLD`, made by R354 Vishay CRCW25122R20FKEGHP (2.2 Ω, 1.5 W pulse-proof 2512), D301 B340A Schottky, and C340 Panasonic [EEUFR1A472S](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/aluminum-cap-lead/models/EEUFR1A472S), 4700 µF ±20%, 10 V, 16 mm diameter × 20 mm body length, 7.5 mm pitch. Its maximum seated height is 22 mm. The diode prevents the reservoir from discharging into `V5_SYS` and the relay/converter when the adapter disappears. R354 limits a simple worst-case initial charge estimate to ~2.1 A at 5 V (about 10 W peak, 10.3 ms RC, and 0.052 J for the idealized pulse); [Vishay’s pulse-proof series](https://www.vishay.com/docs/20043/crcwhpe3.pdf) is selected, but its actual repetitive hot-plug pulse, adapter interaction, and thermal margin still require qualification. At the provisional 110 mA DAC/control allowance, R354 drops 0.24 V in steady operation. U334 [TPS3808G50](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf) is powered from `V5_HOLD` but senses upstream `V5_SYS`; its 4.65 V nominal threshold gives an early loss indication while the hold capacitor remains charged. CT to VDD through 100 kΩ selects the datasheet's ~300 ms reset-release delay at startup. The supervisor's open-drain output `ADAPTER_OK` is pulled up to `V5_HOLD`.

On power-up, U335 buffers `ADAPTER_OK` through two SN74HCS14 inverters. R352 now feeds only a logic input, so the three hold networks no longer load the supervisor output. R365 limits their charge current. U333 generates `EN_D` and the C/A hold qualifiers. U330 uses the existing 100 kΩ/22 nF rising networks to stage AVDD, VCCA, AVCC, then `DAC_EN`. U336 SN74LVC2G08 combines the rising and hold qualifiers for `EN_C` and `EN_A`; 10 kΩ pulldowns bound disabled-chip leakage. This replaces diode-AND enables that could not guarantee the TPS7A20's 0.3 V low threshold. The HCS part has tighter Schmitt thresholds than the previous HC selection. See [TI HCS14](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hcs14.pdf) and [LVC2G08](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g08.pdf).

On adapter loss, buffered supervisor output clamps `PWR_GOOD` and `DAC_EN` before the held enables fall. The current 100 kΩ × **2.2/10/47 nF** networks turn off A, C, D respectively. Source-derived conditional corner bounds are A 0.125–0.357 ms, C 0.566–1.622 ms, D 2.662–7.623 ms. The calculation sweeps common hold voltage, independent resistor/capacitor/threshold corners and leakage assumptions; it also checks interrupted startup. Its assumptions and exact topology are executable in `verification/power_checks.py`. These are enable-order results. The LDO's 150 Ω discharge resistance is only typical, so real rail-voltage trajectories remain a bench gate.

The provisional load budget remains 40 mA D + 20 mA C + 50 mA A, with another 5 mA for control/leakage in the validation model. C340 at −20%, R354 at +1%, the supervisor's low threshold, and the stated 0.5 V hold-diode bound give an initial hold voltage of 3.801 V and a calculated minimum of 3.696 V. This is **96 mV above 3.6 V**, the input required by the TPS7A20 full-regulation specification. The earlier 3.445 V estimate used the dropout number measured at 95% output and was not a full-regulation guarantee. Actual DAC current, diode drop, effective capacitance, temperature, aging and regulator ramps must satisfy the assumptions before hardware acceptance. The manufacturer does not publish a guaranteed mode-8/384 kHz maximum DAC current.

[power-validation.md](power-validation.md) is the current quantitative record. The 22 mm maximum seated reservoir height plus 4 mm standoff, 1.6 mm PCB and 2 mm top allowance remains 29.6 mm within the 32 mm floorplan limit. Exact rail ordering, adapter hot-unplug, inrush and abrupt rail faults require oscilloscope checks on a dummy-load prototype.

## Rail qualification and verification status

U331/U332 LM393 open-collector outputs wire-AND into `PWR_GOOD`, pulled up to `3V3_D`. At nominal supply, thresholds are about `VPOS > +8.09 V`, `VNEG < −8.02 V`, and each later 3.3 V DAC rail >~3.14 V. R339/R341 set the negative sense input near 1.19 V when `VNEG = 0`, within the LM393 full-temperature input common-mode limit (`3V3_D − 2 V`); D349 BAT54WS clamps that input against a negative converter rail while `3V3_D` is absent. [TI's LM393 input minimum](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm393.pdf) is −0.3 V; the [BAT54WS-7-F](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/BAT54WS.pdf) has 0.24 V maximum forward drop at 0.1 mA at 25 °C, while this divider drives <35 µA. Verify cold clamp voltage as well. The later-rail references track `3V3_D`, so this is fault detection, not an absolute ESS minimum-voltage guarantee. A BAT54WS clamp also forces `PWR_GOOD` low on `ADAPTER_OK` loss. `DAC_EN` is diode-ANDed with the staged enable, `PWR_GOOD`, and `ADAPTER_OK`; it goes low first. The DAC mute circuit should use `DAC_EN` and `PWR_GOOD` as immediate qualifiers.

The existing KiCad 10 library was parsed for every component: all symbol pins are mapped or explicitly NC and all mapped pins have footprint pads. The integrated source and generated schematic now pass static checks and ERC; this library check does not itself establish routed DRC or electrical sign-off. Power ICs have `spice: false` because no qualified vendor models were integrated. Before manufacture, run integrated ERC and routing/DRC; select exact capacitor MPNs under DC bias; verify jack contacts and adapter polarity; measure startup/shutdown sequence, hold capacitor current and inrush, ±9 V ripple/sidebands, hot-plug and brownout behavior, USB-connected/adapter-off back-power paths, and output mute/relay timing. A layout PASS does not verify these behaviors.
