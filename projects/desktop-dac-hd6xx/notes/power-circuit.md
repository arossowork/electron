# Rev A power circuit (component-level candidate)

`design.yaml` contains the integrated circuit, not a released board. Its 109 power-section components expose `V5_SYS`, `VPOS`, `VNEG`, `3V3_D`, `3V3_C`, `3V3_A`, `DAC_EN`, and `PWR_GOOD`. The external adapter must supply regulated, center-positive 5 V; there is no mains on the board. The USB receiver is separately USB-powered with common `GND`. The analog and USB/DAC fragments own `AMP_ENABLE` and DAC mute, respectively.

## Power paths

J300 (CUI PJ-102AH) enters F300 (1.5 A hold PTC), M300 (AO3401A reverse-polarity P-MOS), and `V5_SYS`. D300 is a transient suppressor. M300's drain is on the fused input, source on `V5_SYS`, and gate pulled low. The [AO3401A datasheet](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO3401A.pdf) gives at most 60 mΩ at −4.5 V gate drive; the relay's [178 Ω ±10% coil](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g6k.pdf) draws 28.1 mA nominal and 31.2 mA at its low-resistance limit at 5 V. Budget at least 35 mA on `V5_SYS` for that relay. The TVS does not regulate sustained overvoltage, and its transient clamp exceeds the 6 V absolute maximum of some downstream parts. Qualify the adapter, inrush, and fault survival.

U310 [TPS65131](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps65131.pdf) converts `V5_SYS` to about +10.5 V/−10 V raw with 4.7 µH inductors, Schottky diodes, TI example feedback values (1 MΩ/130 kΩ positive; 1 MΩ/121 kΩ negative), and TI's 6.8 pF/7.5 pF feed-forward capacitors. `PSP` and `PSN` low select forced PWM. Output capacitance is 2 × 10 µF, 25 V, per raw rail; exact MPN and effective capacitance under bias remain to be selected. `BSW` is left open per TI's option without a battery-disconnect switch, so the positive raw output is not fully disconnected in converter shutdown.

**QFN pad escape.** U310 is available in TI's 0.5 mm-pitch RGE VQFN package. Its ~0.25 mm-wide pads and this board's 0.20 mm clearance leave at most `2 × (0.5 − 0.125 − 0.20) = 0.35 mm` for a straight escape between unlike pad nets. Set `board.power_track_mm: 0.30` in the integrated design, keeping `V5_SYS.current_a: 1.5` and each raw rail's `current_a: 0.15` unchanged: the latter route at 0.30 mm, while the 1.5 A main bus still calculates to 0.6 mm and still needs a local neck at its U310 pad. The compiler allows up to 3 mm per local neck and warns on track-current shortfall, but the DSL cannot specify an exact per-pad neck length/shape. [TI's layout guidance](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps65131.pdf) requires wide, short main current paths and close input/output capacitors, inductors, and diodes; verify the actual routed neck, capacitor loops, peak/ripple current, and copper temperature before release. A 0.30 mm global power-class minimum does not waive any net's stated `current_a` or a larger explicit minimum.

U311 [TPS7A3901](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a39.pdf) post-regulates to approximately +9.09 V and −8.93 V: `1.188 × (1 + 66.5/10)` and `−1.19 × 75/10`. Nominal headroom is ~1.4 V/+rail and ~1.1 V/−rail. Crucially, **U311 EN is tied to its positive input `P10V5_RAW`**, not 5 V: TI requires EN not to exceed VINP and recommends connecting EN to VINP when no separate control is used. Each regulated output is rated 150 mA. The two 4.7 µH inductors are Sunlord MWSA0503S-4R7MT, matching the footprint and the [manufacturer's series data](https://www.sunlordinc.com/uploads/files/20230303/MWSA-S%C2%A0series%C2%A0of%C2%A0SMD%C2%A0Power%C2%A0Inductor.pdf). Full analog load, heat, and switching-noise margin are still unmeasured.

## DAC start and ordered adapter-loss shutdown

Three fixed, low-noise [TPS7A2033PDBVR](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a20.pdf) regulators feed ESS AVDD (`3V3_D`), VCCA (`3V3_C`), and AVCC_DAC1/2 (`3V3_A`). All three take input from `V5_HOLD`, made by R354 Vishay CRCW25122R20FKEGHP (2.2 Ω, 1.5 W pulse-proof 2512), D301 B340A Schottky, and C340 Panasonic [EEUFR1A472S](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/aluminum-cap-lead/models/EEUFR1A472S), 4700 µF ±20%, 10 V, 16 mm diameter × 20 mm body length, 7.5 mm pitch. Its maximum seated height is 22 mm. The diode prevents the reservoir from discharging into `V5_SYS` and the relay/converter when the adapter disappears. R354 limits a simple worst-case initial charge estimate to ~2.1 A at 5 V (about 10 W peak, 10.3 ms RC, and 0.052 J for the idealized pulse); [Vishay’s pulse-proof series](https://www.vishay.com/docs/20043/crcwhpe3.pdf) is selected, but its actual repetitive hot-plug pulse, adapter interaction, and thermal margin still require qualification. At the provisional 110 mA DAC/control allowance, R354 drops 0.24 V in steady operation. U334 [TPS3808G50](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf) is powered from `V5_HOLD` but senses upstream `V5_SYS`; its 4.65 V nominal threshold gives an early loss indication while the hold capacitor remains charged. CT to VDD through 100 kΩ selects the datasheet's ~300 ms reset-release delay at startup. The supervisor's open-drain output `ADAPTER_OK` is pulled up to `V5_HOLD`.

On power-up, `ADAPTER_OK` enables U320 (AVDD). U330 74HC14 with 100 kΩ/22 nF rising RCs enables U321 (VCCA) only after AVDD, U322 (AVCC) only after VCCA, and finally `DAC_EN` after AVCC. Each nominal RC time constant is 2.2 ms, greater than the ESS [~200 µs AVDD-to-VCCA recommendation](https://www.esstech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ES9039Q2M_Datasheet_v0.2.3.pdf). `PWR_GOOD` also qualifies `DAC_EN`.

When `V5_SYS` falls below the supervisor threshold, its RESET output immediately clamps `PWR_GOOD` and `DAC_EN` low. U333 74HC14, powered from `V5_HOLD`, uses three separately charged RC nodes to hold LDO enables high briefly and then drop **A, C, D** in that order. Nominal estimates from their 100 kΩ × 4.7/22/100 nF networks are about 0.5/2.4/10 ms after `ADAPTER_OK` falls; actual trip times vary with 74HC14 thresholds and part tolerance. The `P` suffix on all three TPS7A20 MPNs selects TI's active output-discharge variant (150 Ω typical); the reservoir keeps their inputs alive while they discharge. This is a concrete ordered-shutdown circuit, not a demonstrated voltage-waveform guarantee. The [SN74HC14 switching thresholds](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hc14.pdf) span a wide range: simple 4.5 V threshold bounds give roughly 0.25–0.72 ms (A), 1.2–3.4 ms (C), and 5.4–15.4 ms (D), before RC tolerances. The nominal EN ordering is thus separated, but component tolerances and actual output-discharge waveforms can narrow the gap. The output rail voltage order, EN low levels, hold-up margin, and adapter hot-unplug/brownout transitions need oscilloscope qualification at load and temperature. An abrupt rail short can defeat the recommended ESS order and is a separate protection case.

The [ESS v0.2.3 power table](https://www.esstech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ES9039Q2M_Datasheet_v0.2.3.pdf) provides **typical** current only for several modes, not a maximum or a mode-8/384 kHz value. The largest shown entries sum to ~23.5 mA (9.4 mA AVDD, 0.5 mA VCCA, 6.8 mA on each AVCC). For capacitor sizing, allow a provisional 40 mA D + 20 mA C + 50 mA A = 110 mA total, over four times that published typical sum. Integrating those allowances over nominal 0.5/2.4/10 ms shutdown windows gives ~0.47 mC; at C340's −20% limit (3760 µF), estimated reservoir drop is ~0.13 V. Using a ~17 ms extended D window increases this estimate to ~0.20 V. With TPS3808G50's 4.65 V nominal trip and −2% threshold tolerance, a conservative 0.5 V Schottky drop, and R354's 0.24 V at 110 mA, `V5_HOLD` can begin shutdown near 3.82 V and reach about 3.62 V at the extended-window estimate. The [TPS7A20](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a20.pdf) lists 145 mV maximum dropout at 300 mA in its DBV package, placing the nominal 3.3 V regulation requirement around 3.445 V. The calculated margin is therefore ~175 mV under these assumptions; colder/lower-capacitance parts, capacitor aging, higher DAC current, or a larger diode drop could remove it. This is **not** a datasheet maximum-current proof. Measure mode-8 current at maximum sample rate, timing spread, initial diode drop, and rail discharge before accepting C340 or R354. If the measured current or worst-case margin is larger than budgeted, shorten the D hold interval with a demonstrated rail-order margin or choose more reservoir capacitance that fits the enclosure. The capacitor's 22 mm maximum seated height plus provisional 4 mm standoff, 1.6 mm PCB, and 2 mm top allowance totals 29.6 mm within the 32 mm floorplan limit; its 16 mm diameter demands a footprint-level packing check.

## Rail qualification and verification status

U331/U332 LM393 open-collector outputs wire-AND into `PWR_GOOD`, pulled up to `3V3_D`. At nominal supply, thresholds are about `VPOS > +8.09 V`, `VNEG < −8.02 V`, and each later 3.3 V DAC rail >~3.14 V. R339/R341 set the negative sense input near 1.19 V when `VNEG = 0`, within the LM393 full-temperature input common-mode limit (`3V3_D − 2 V`); D349 BAT54WS clamps that input against a negative converter rail while `3V3_D` is absent. [TI's LM393 input minimum](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm393.pdf) is −0.3 V; the [BAT54WS-7-F](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/BAT54WS.pdf) has 0.24 V maximum forward drop at 0.1 mA at 25 °C, while this divider drives <35 µA. Verify cold clamp voltage as well. The later-rail references track `3V3_D`, so this is fault detection, not an absolute ESS minimum-voltage guarantee. A BAT54WS clamp also forces `PWR_GOOD` low on `ADAPTER_OK` loss. `DAC_EN` is diode-ANDed with the staged enable, `PWR_GOOD`, and `ADAPTER_OK`; it goes low first. The DAC mute circuit should use `DAC_EN` and `PWR_GOOD` as immediate qualifiers.

The existing KiCad 10 library was parsed for every component: all symbol pins are mapped or explicitly NC and all mapped pins have footprint pads. This is **not** ERC, SPICE, routed DRC, or electrical signoff. Power ICs have `spice: false` because no qualified vendor models were integrated. Before manufacture, run integrated ERC and routing/DRC; select exact capacitor MPNs under DC bias; verify jack contacts and adapter polarity; measure startup/shutdown sequence, hold capacitor current and inrush, ±9 V ripple/sidebands, hot-plug and brownout behavior, USB-connected/adapter-off back-power paths, and output mute/relay timing. A layout PASS does not verify these behaviors.
