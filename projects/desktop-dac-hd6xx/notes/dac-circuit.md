# USB receiver and DAC circuit, revision A (2026-10-24)

The electrical capture is [`design.yaml`](../design.yaml); the project-local KiCad symbols are [`lib/symbols/dac_audio.kicad_sym`](../lib/symbols/dac_audio.kicad_sym). This is an integrated circuit source; its current ERC and connectivity checks are in `circuit-baseline.md`. It is not a routed or measured audio board. Active USB/DAC/logic parts are marked `spice: false` because no qualified device-level audio/clock model is present; analog performance is not implied by static net connectivity.

## Sources and exact pin mapping

- [Amanero Combo768 Rev A datasheet, 04/08/2025](https://www.amanero.com/drivers/Combo768-D.pdf), output connector table p.2: module is USB bus-powered here. J100 is its 2×10, 2.54-mm interface, with pin 3 data, 4 bit clock, 5 frame sync, 6 MCLK, 7 DSDOE (high on DSD), 11 MUTE (high on sample-rate or DSD-mode change), 1 USB-cable-present (high when plugged), and 8/13/14/15 ground. Header pins 9/10 are the module's 3.3-V auxiliary output, **50 mA maximum together**; both are deliberately NC so host USB power is not tied to the external adapter's DAC rails. Pin 2/12 are reserved in the table, while the drawing labels SCL/SDA; both are NC. Pin 16 needs special firmware, and pins 17–20 are rate indicators; all are NC. The connector footprint's electrical 1–20 pads were checked, but the actual module's mating gender, header orientation, USB-C location and support height still need its mechanical drawing.
- [ESS ES9039Q2M datasheet v0.2.3, May 2026](https://www.esstech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ES9039Q2M_Datasheet_v0.2.3.pdf), Table 1 pp.11–12 and Figure 31 p.96: U100 pin 14 DATA2 receives I²S data, pin 13 DATA1 receives WS/LRCLK, pin 12 DATA_CLK receives BCLK, and pin 32 receives MCLK. Pins 3/2 are left positive/negative analog outputs; 7/6 are right positive/negative. These are **differential DAC outputs**, not line-level headphone signals. The separate OPA1612 output stage is owned by the analog circuit and must define gain, filtering and common-mode handling before the volume pot.
- ESS Table 7 p.16: hardware mode 8 is MODE pin 29 `Pull 0` (R100 47 kΩ to ground), HW2 pin 19 `Pull 1` (R101 47 kΩ to AVDD), HW1 pin 15 and HW0 pin 16 directly grounded. It is synchronous, 32-bit I²S slave with automatic sample-rate detection and **64 × fs BCLK**. The module's documented header MCLK is 22.5792 or 24.576 MHz, subject to firmware/configuration. MCLK/fs ratios are 512/256/128/64 for 44.1/88.2/176.4/352.8 kHz and 48/96/192/384 kHz respectively. At 384 kHz, BCLK = 24.576 MHz and MCLK = 24.576 MHz, meeting mode 8's 64 × fs minimum and 49.152-MHz maximum. This arithmetic does not verify that the module actually emits 64 × fs BCLK and correct 32-bit stereo framing at every rate; capture those four signals on a prototype before claiming 384-kHz system support. 705.6/768 kHz cannot use this module MCLK with mode 8 because 64 × fs would exceed the documented 22.5792/24.576-MHz MCLK.

The U100 symbol was adapted from this repository's earlier ES9039Q2M symbol and checked against ESS Table 1: 33 numbered terminals including exposed pad 33. KiCad's `QFN-32-1EP_5x5mm_P0.5mm_EP3.3x3.3mm` has pads 1–33 at 0.5-mm pitch; ESS Figure 37 p.104 gives a 5×5-mm package, 0.5-mm pitch and 3.20-mm nominal exposed pad. Its 3.3-mm land, paste openings and ground-via pattern need assembly review. All 35 components in the fragment have each symbol pin explicitly connected or listed NC, and all selected footprints were checked for matching numbered pads via `hwflow` library calls. The TI custom logic symbols follow manufacturer pin tables (links below), not assumed 74xx equivalence.

## Power, mute and signal boundaries

U100 AVDD pin 10 uses 3V3_D, VCCA pin 31 uses 3V3_C, AVCC_DAC1/2 pins 1/8 use 3V3_A, and the *internally generated* 1.2-V DVDD pin 9 only gets its 1-µF capacitor; it is not tied to an external rail. C100/C101 are 1 µF at AVDD/VCCA. C102/C103/C104 are 1-µF, 10-V KEMET [T491A105K010AT](https://content.kemet.com/datasheets/KEM_T2005_T491.pdf) tantalum at AVCC_DAC1, AVCC_DAC2 and DVDD with positive pad to the named supply, as in ESS Figure 31; the specific 3216 KiCad footprint pads were checked. Pins 4/5/11/30/33, reserved RT1 pin 17 and GPIO7 pin 27 are grounded. GPIO5 pin 25 is low to disable DoP, GPIO6 pin 26 low selects the default minimum-phase FIR. Unused GPIO1–4 and GPIO8 are NC. GPIO2 is not used as a readiness detector: ESS says SRC lock status is 0 in synchronous mode (p.24).

[ESS recommended sequence](https://www.esstech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ES9039Q2M_Datasheet_v0.2.3.pdf), p.51: raise AVDD, about 200 µs later VCCA, then AVCC_DAC1/2, then CHIP_EN; power down in reverse. The power circuit owns that sequence and `DAC_EN`. ESS Figure 4 p.18 recommends MUTE_CTRL low until hardware mode is set after CHIP_EN, then unmute last. The circuit makes `AUDIO_READY = PWR_GOOD ∧ USB_CABLE ∧ ¬USB_MUTE ∧ ¬USB_DSDOE ∧ DAC_EN ∧ delayed(DAC_EN)`. `PWR_GOOD` is the power circuit's open-collector high permission, with R105 local fail-low pulldown; `DAC_EN` also has a fail-low pulldown. R106/C105 (22 kΩ/1 µF) and two [SN74LVC14A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc14a.pdf) Schmitt stages delay assertion by a threshold-dependent roughly 7–21 ms at nominal 3.3 V (calculated from TI's 0.9–2.0-V rising threshold at 3.0–3.6 V); component/rail tolerance broadens that range. D100 with R118 (2.2 kΩ, 1%) discharges the timing node when DAC_EN falls. This was increased from 100 Ω after review traced the discharge through D348 to the TPS3808 RESET sink, whose specified limit is 5 mA. At 3.465 V and the resistor’s −1% limit, ignoring diode drops conservatively bounds the capacitor transient at 1.59 mA; R106 contributes at most about 0.16 mA and the three 10 kΩ pull-ups about 1.25 mA under the stated supply assumptions, totaling about 3 mA. Nominal discharge RC is 2.2 ms. Immediate DAC_EN/PWR_GOOD mute terms do not wait for this discharge; re-arm timing remains a waveform-validation gate. [SN74LV21A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lv21a.pdf) provides the two 4-input AND stages; DAC_EN and PWR_GOOD are immediate terms, independent of the delay. The final Schmitt stage drives M100 BSS138: when `AUDIO_READY` is low it clamps DAC_MUTE_N/pin 20 to ground; when high, R102's 47-kΩ pull-up to AVDD un-mutes it and requests ESS hardware automute (Table 6 p.15). Verify this Pull-1/direct-0 transition during bench startup and stream changes. `AUDIO_READY` is a logic permission to the analog amplifier protection circuit, **not proof of DAC lock, output DC safety or relay closure**.

U103 [SN74LVC244A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc244a.pdf) buffers all four I²S lines from the USB-powered module to the adapter-powered DAC. TI specifies powered-off Ioff/back-drive protection; its unused bank is disabled with inputs grounded. DAC_EN pulls the active bank's OE low through a Schmitt inverter only when the DAC is enabled; R107 pulls OE high by default. R108–R111 are 33-Ω source-series pads immediately after the buffer. R112–R117 hold module signals at defined low levels when the header is absent. The buffer's additive clock jitter and four-line timing skew have **not** been measured; verify at U100 pins, especially 384 kHz, before a performance claim. The module's own 47-Ω output specification is on its header, upstream of this buffer.

No ES9312 calibration regulator is included. ESS p.48 says its GPIO8 calibration resistor can compensate DAC output-impedance variation when paired with a controlled AVCC supply; the chosen plain low-noise 3V3_A rail keeps GPIO8 NC and cannot claim calibrated output-level consistency across chips. Confirm the output stage's real full-scale voltage, noise and distortion rather than applying the datasheet's evaluation-board 130-dB/−120-dB numbers to this circuit.

## Gates before layout/fabrication

The circuit is integrated and its current source/ERC and exported-netlist checks are recorded in `circuit-baseline.md`. Continue electrical qualification before routing. Reconcile regulator rail order, its actual `PWR_GOOD` assertion/fall times, and DAC_EN with this mute logic. Check bus-powered USB versus adapter-powered DAC in both power-connection orders, including an absent module. Confirm the Amanero configuration produces I²S 64 × fs at 44.1–384 kHz and mute/DSDOE polarity, and measure clocks after U103. Check the output stage, pot/amplifier and relay/dummy-load startup transient before attaching headphones. The static checks do not establish fabrication readiness or audio performance.

## Integrated schematic correction

KiCad ERC found that the original custom SN74LVC244A drawing placed pin 19 (2OE, tied high) and pin 17 (unused input, grounded) at the same graphical endpoint. This created a generated-schematic short despite distinct source nets. Pin 19 was moved to its own endpoint; its electrical number and function are unchanged. The issue was in the project symbol, not a legitimate supply connection or a reason to suppress ERC. The integrated validation record includes the regenerated ERC result.
