# Simulation model provenance and limits

The two TI-derived amplifier libraries were copied without modification from the repository's existing `projects/mechanical-keyboard-usb-audio-hub/lib/spice/` models. Their retained headers identify the manufacturer releases and the previous PSpice-to-ngspice syntax translation (switches, temperature, conditional expressions and noiseless resistors). This project has not requalified that translation. Manufacturer sources: [OPA1622](https://www.ti.com/product/OPA1622) and [OPA1612](https://www.ti.com/product/OPA1612). Copyright/model notices are preserved.

`analog_wrappers.lib` maps two single-amplifier models onto each dual physical package. OPA1622 EN, GND and exposed-pad behavior are not modeled. The LM339 model is a behavioral open-collector threshold approximation without real input limits, offset, delay or saturation behavior. Generic diode/transistor models also cannot qualify protection behavior. USB, DAC, power and digital logic ICs marked `spice: false` have no qualified functional model here. No simulation result is currently used as product verification.

The direct single-amplifier reproduction is `verification/opa1622-op.cir`. From that directory, run `timeout 20s ngspice -b opa1622-op.cir` in the pinned image. The equivalent original isolation run returned exit 124 after 20.8 s, following failed gmin/source stepping, without a trustworthy operating point. KLU had the same result. This is a model/solver integration blocker, not evidence of amplifier hardware failure. Resolve it with a qualified model translation or another validated simulator before relying on the complete-board gain bench. Do not replace the model with an ideal amplifier and claim equivalent validation.

The `design.yaml` gain bench injects ideal ±9 V rails and two 2 Vrms sources at the volume-wiper nets. It measures the pre-relay amplifier outputs into 300 Ω loads. Other modeled parts remain in the netlist, but the DAC, logic and supply conversion are absent as functional devices; this cannot establish complete-system behavior. Its analysis timeout is 30 s. Rail sequencing, DAC full scale, protection, relay release, clipping, noise and stability need separate qualified tests; Monte Carlo is deferred until nominal simulations are valid.

## Bounded alternate-vendor-model probe

The official [TI TINA-TI SBOM957C archive](https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/SBOM957C) was also retrieved. ZIP SHA256 `52a828e5b55e5c5c148f1fdc7d9327e362eabda1f37e8ea1a43d739568b9189b`; extracted `OPA1622.LIB` SHA256 `9ef9e9cf6e648b859412e2ecbc0cd2f2304587b1d7687805e90112935e197a45`. Its header identifies Final 1.4, 03DEC2021, and the same five-port amplifier interface. It is a distinct source from the inherited Final 1.5 PSpice translation. One minimal single-channel ngspice compatibility probe exited during parsing on `Undefined parameter [temp]` / `Cannot compute substitute`, also reporting switch-model mismatches. It produced no transient result. The TINA file is not a drop-in ngspice replacement and was not substituted into this circuit. The named disposable probe container was confirmed removed afterward.
