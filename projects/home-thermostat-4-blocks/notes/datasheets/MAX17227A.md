## Absolute maximum ratings

IN, EN, RSEL, OUT to GND -0.3 to +6 V; LX RMS current +-1.6 A RMS; IN RMS current +-0.8 A RMS; output short circuit
continuous; TJ -40 to +125 C operating, 150 C max; TDFN 937.9 mW at 70 C (derate 11.7 mW/C); theta-JA 85.3 C/W (4-layer).
Reversed cells: IN abs min -0.3 V, the same as the TPS61299 (R4 guard keeps VBAT_P >= about -0.3 V; the reverse_* sims
check it).

IN RMS 0.8 A: in boost operation the inductor current flows LX -> OUT, not through IN (IN supplies the control); IN
carries current in the short-circuit / pass-through path (limited to 1.1 A typ). With the guard OFF and 3.7 V lithium
cells, VBAT_P <= 3.3 V (body diodes) so the part boosts or idles; check the IN RMS rating against the bench pass-through
current if pass-through occurs.

## Recommended circuit (Design Procedure, Typical Application)

- L = 1.0 uH (DFE201610E-1R0M kept: Isat 2.7 A against the 2.2 A max peak limit, DCR 57 mOhm).
- CIN: at minimum a standard 22 uF ceramic (X7R recommended); more for sources above 1 ohm (here C1 + C2 6.6 mF bulk on VBAT_P).
  C3 changed to 22 uF 25 V X5R 0805 (C45783, basic).
- COUT: 22 uF recommended, minimum 11 uF effective over temperature and DC bias; "In applications where VIN is approaching
  VOUT, more output capacitance will be required" (Output Capacitor, p.19). Since 2026-10-22: C4 + C5 = 2 x 47 uF
  10 V X5R 1206 (CL31A476MPHNNNE, C96123, basic): 26.3 uF each at 3.4 V (Samsung DC-bias curve, -44 %), 52.6 uF effective
  (notes/circuit.md, delta review). CIN: the data sheet also asks for more input capacitance when VIN approaches VOUT
  (Input Capacitor); C3 stays 1 x 22 uF (15 uF effective at 3.3 V, Samsung curve -32 %), see notes/circuit.md.
- RSEL 191 k 1 %, trace capacitance < 2 pF.
- Layout: COUT next to the IC (most important), short COUT ground to GND pin, minimal LX area, GND and AGND to EP
  with short traces under the device.
