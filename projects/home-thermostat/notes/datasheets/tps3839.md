Consequences for the design:
- The old model values (150 us delay, 2.87 V) were wrong: the delay is 200 ms (120-350). EN now stays low 0.12-0.35 s after the 3V3 rail passes 2.96 V (max 3.003 V, just above the ESP32 3.0 V minimum), which is a start-up delay, not a problem. The R5/C6 RC on EN adds 14 ms on top.
- Worst-case sleep cost is 500 nA, not 150 nA (+0.35 uA at the rail, about +0.5 uA at the pack). R2 margin is 195.8 uW vs the 208 uW limit.
- Release can occur at 2.886 V (min corner) before the 200 ms delay ends: the rail keeps rising during the delay, so EN is never released below 3.0 V only if the boost output reaches 3.0 V within 120 ms of crossing 2.886 V. With 4.7 mF at the 200 mA start-up limit this holds (tens of ms); check in the powerup simulation.
- SPICE: TI publishes PSpice transient models for TPS3839K33 as SBVM235 (https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/SBVM235, files TPS3839K33_TRANS.lib/.DSN/.OLB). It could not be unpacked in the parts stage (no extraction tool permitted), so `models/tps3839_beh.lib` stays behavioral with the datasheet values above (spice.source: behavioral).
