## Stage 3 re-check (iteration 3): pad positions

Two more TE datasheet copies read (Rev 0717 and Rev 0411, p.2, "PCB layout / terminal assignment, bottom view on solder pins", 1 form C): holes dia 1.3 +0.1 on a "2.5-2.54" mm grid in both directions, 1.4 mm from the body edge; terminal labels A1, 11, 14 in the top row, A2, 12 in the bottom row. Scaling the figure (body 20 mm = 88 px): A1-A2 about 7.3 mm (7.5 grid), A1-11 about 9.5 mm (10), A1-14 about 16 mm (15 to 17.5). The PROVISIONAL footprint (A1 0,0; A2 0,7.5; 11 10,0; 14 15,0; 12 15,7.5) agrees with the figure to one grid step but is NOT verified: the figure has no pin-to-pin dimensions in mm. NOT renamed; still blocks layout. The DXF/STEP on https://www.te.com/en/product-1415390-1.html needs a TE download that WebFetch cannot make.
TE page status: "This product is not currently available" (te.com, 2026-10-19). Procurement risk: Buerklin, Farnell and Distrelec list 1415390-1, stock not read. KiCad has no PE014 footprint (kicad-footprints PR #2225 for PE01xFxx is open and abandoned; reviewer asks 3 mm pads and pins 1/2 at 1.4 mm from the left border, no coordinates).
Fallback: Panasonic DE1A-L-3V (not on LCSC, pinout unknown, see parts.md).

## Lead check (iteration 3): footprint corrected
Read the 04/25 datasheets ("Schrack Miniature PCB Relay PE" p.3, larger figure, and "PE bistable" p.3, both TE 2025). Grid counts from the 1 form C figure: A1-11 = 4 grid (figure 10.2 mm), 11-14 = 3 grid (7.1 mm), A1-14 = 7 grid (17.3 mm), row pitch 3 grid (7.3 mm); the right holes sit about 1.1-1.6 mm from the right body edge, which matches 1.4 + 17.5 = 18.9 of a 20 mm body (15 mm would leave 3.6 mm). Bottom view: A1, 11, 14 top row; A2, 12 bottom row.
New footprint `thermostat:Relay_TE_PE014_Bistable` (top view = bottom view mirrored in Y): A2 (0,0), 12 (17.5,0), A1 (0,7.5), 11 (10,7.5), 14 (17.5,7.5), drill 1.3. The provisional one had 14/12 at 15 mm and was not mirrored. Residual risk: 2.5 vs 2.54 mm grid (0.28 mm over 7 grid, inside the 1.3 mm hole tolerance for the ~0.5 mm pins); check one real relay against a 1:1 print before ordering boards.
