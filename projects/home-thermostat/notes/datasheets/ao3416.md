Reverse-cell case: VGS < 0 (gate on the cell +), channel off, body diode reverse biased; IDSS 1 uA max at 20 V is the leakage bound. Reverse VGS must stay within -8 V (two reversed AAA = -3.6 V, ok).
Voltage drop at 0.65 A pack current, 34 mOhm max: 22 mV (plus 17 mOhm typ at 25 C = 14 mV).
Alternatives (not read): Vishay Si2312CDS (Vth 0.4-1.0 V, RDS about 0.048 ohm at 1.8 V from memory, verify), Diodes DMN2056U.
Model: `models/nmos_lowvgs.lib` NMOS_LOWVGS (typ) and NMOS_LOWVGS_MAX (max), spice.source: fit. AOS has no SPICE model on the datasheet page checked.
