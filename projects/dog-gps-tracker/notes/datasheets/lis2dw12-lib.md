# LIS2DW12 library facts (DS11811 Rev 9, mouser/semic mirror of the ST PDF; st.com blocks fetch)
- Table 1 p.4 (read as text and Figure 2 bottom view as image): 1 SCL/SPC, 2 CS (pulled up, 20-54 kohm), 3 SDO/SA0 (pulled up), 4 SDA/SDI/SDO, 5 NC (can be tied to VDD/VDDIO/GND), 6 GND, 7 RES (connect to GND), 8 GND, 9 VDD, 10 VDD_IO, 11 INT2, 12 INT1. Symbol `dog_misc:LIS2DW12`.
- Abs max Table 9 p.? : Vdd and Vdd_IO -0.3..4.8 V, Vin -0.3..Vdd_IO+0.3, ESD 2 kV HBM, T -40..85 C. Package LGA-12L 2.0 x 2.0 x 0.7 max (+-0.1 / +-0.15), Fig 18 p.56. Land pattern is NOT in the datasheet (TN0018, not fetched).
- Footprint: KiCad Package_LGA:LGA-12_2x2mm_P0.5mm: 12 pads 0.375 x 0.35 pitch 0.5, left side 1-4, bottom 5-6, right 7-10, top 11-12 (counter-clockwise from the top left). ST bottom view (pin 1 top right, 2-4 down the right) mirrored to the PCB view gives pin 1 top left, 2-4 down the left, 5-6 along the bottom left to right, 7-10 up the right, 11-12 along the top: matches the KiCad pad numbers exactly. The pad size is KiCad's, not ST's TN0018: keep as reuse (symbol footprint field points to the KiCad footprint).
