# CAP-XX GW209F (dual-cell prismatic supercap, 160 mF)
Sources: datasheet GW109/GW209 rev 4.5 (2020) https://cap-xx-assets.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/CAP_XX_GW_109_GW_209_Datasheet_1a2a95e958.pdf ; Product Guide 2018 v6.0 https://www.a-tecuk.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/CAP-XX-Product-Guide-2018-v6.0.pdf (pp.3, 12).
- Abs max: Vpeak 5.5 V; Tmax -40..+70 C. Rated Vn 5.0 V (cells 2.5 V each; at 3.6 V rail ~1.8 V per cell: derated, good for life).
- C (DC, 23 C): 128 / 160 / 192 mF (+-20 %). ESR (DC, 23 C): 55 typ / 66 max mOhm. ESR at -40 C ~2x, at 70 C ~0.8x (Fig 7, p.5): -20 C assumed ~1.7x = ~112 mOhm max. Capacitance at -20 C: not in text (Fig). Not stated -> bench.
- Leakage: 0.5 / 1 uA (2.3 V per cell, 23 C, after 120 h); decays over time, 120 h to equilibrium (higher at first charge); ~5 uA at 70 C (Fig 10, p.7). Estimate ~2 uA at 50 C. At 3.6 V (1.8 V/cell) lower than the 2.3 V figure.
- IRMS 6 A, peak 30 A non-repetitive. Pulse: Vdrop = I x ESR + I x t / C.
- Thickness 2.1 mm (GW209F, no tape), 2.2 mm (GW209G with adhesive tape underside). Body 28.5 x 17.0 mm (guide table p.3, GW series). Mass: not in any datasheet (TME gross 1.08 g incl. packaging); arch estimate 2.0 g.
- Assembly (guide p.12): NOT suitable for IR/hot-air reflow or wave; secondary operation by manual iron (<70 W, <= 5 s at 400 C), hot bar, conductive adhesive, ultrasonic or laser welding. Solder terminals only, never the casing; discharged when soldered; no vias/tracks under the conductive underside areas; < 400 kPa pressure. Cell balancing recommended for dual-cell modules (passive resistors); landing-pad drawing in guide section 20 (not fetched).
- Status: Digikey "Obsolete", Octopart EOL (search summary); CAP-XX site lists it (quote, MOQ 300 in 2018 guide). Price not published.
