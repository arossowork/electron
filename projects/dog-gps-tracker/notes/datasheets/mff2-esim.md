# Truphone SIM-S-IO3-MFF2-2 (ST33G1M2) and the MFF2 package
Sources: Truphone for Things datasheet v2 (media.digikey.com/pdf/Data%20Sheets/Truphone%20PDFs/Truphone_for_Things_SIM_Datasheet_v2_10-27-20.pdf): 5 x 6 x 1.27 mm (Truphone says 1.27 mm; the 1GLOBAL datasheet of Oct 2023 says 0.9 mm: take 1.27 mm as the height until the part is measured), -40..+105 C (MFF2), class A/B/C 5/3/1.8 V. Its pin-out is an image (p.4, not text-readable); the same package in the 1GLOBAL sheet p.4 (Fig 1, VFDFPN8, "compatible with MFF2 of ETSI 102 671 rel 12", read as image) gives: 1 GND, 2 SWIO (VPP, not used), 3 I/O, 4 NC, 5 NC, 6 CLK, 7 RESET, 8 VCC. Package table (1GLOBAL p.5-6): D 6.0 (5.9-6.1), E 5.0 (4.9-5.1), e 1.27, b 0.4 (0.35-0.48), L 0.6 (0.5-0.75), D2 3.4, E2 4.2 (die pad, exposed), A 0.9.
- Library: `dog_misc:MFF2_DFN8_5x6_P1.27` + symbol `dog_misc:SIM-S-IO3-MFF2-2`. The brief said "no exposed pad": the package table shows an exposed die pad (D2 x E2) but the pin table does not connect it; the footprint has NO copper there (pads 0.95 x 0.6 are derived: lead + 0.35 outside, not a vendor land pattern). Confirm with Truphone/ST (ST4SIM-200M data brief, Fig 7 and 12) before fab; no vendor land pattern was found.
- Pin 1 orientation: footprint = PCB view; pins 1-4 down the left, 5-8 up the right (ST top view: 1 top-left, 8 top-right).
- Typical connection (1GLOBAL Fig 2 p.4): VCC + 100 nF, I/O with a pull-up to VCC on the modem side (check the nRF9151 SIM pull-up), RST, CLK direct, GND. Abs max not read.
