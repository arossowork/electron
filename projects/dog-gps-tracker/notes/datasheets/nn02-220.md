## Project library `dog_misc:NN02-220` (stage 3 lib)
- Land pattern: DS_NN02-220 (ignion.io/files/DS_NN02-220.pdf, Apr 2024) sec 2.4 p.4: A=18.0 (inner gap), B=4.0 (gap between the two pads of a side), C=D=3.1 (pad height / width), E=2.0 (feed tab width), tol +-0.05. Pad 1 = feed = lower left (datasheet orientation, ground area below), plus a 2.0 mm wide tab running to the component edge toward the matching network; pads 2-4 mounting, must NOT be grounded (User Manual UM_NN02-220 Dec 2025, Fig 4 p.8; in the manual's bottom view 1 is lower right, mirrored from the PCB view). Pad stack: pads at x = +-(9.0..12.1), y = -6..-2.9 (upper) and 1.1..4.2 (lower); feed tab y 4.2..6.0.
- Pad 1 is two rect objects with the same number (pad + tab): a pad count of 5 against 4 symbol pins is intended.
- Matching network topology (AN Fig 6 p.9, read from the figure): NN02-220 pad 1 - Z1 series L - node 1 (Z2 shunt L to GND) - Z3 series C - node 2 (Z4 shunt C to GND) - node 3 (Z5 shunt L to GND) - Z6 series C - Z7 series L - RF port. Nodes 2 and 3 are one wire (Z4 and Z5 both shunt on the same net) in the figure.
- 60 mm column values (Table 5/6 p.9): Z1 4.7 nH, Z2 13 nH, Z3 1.2 pF, Z4 1.1 pF, Z5 9.1 nH, Z6 2.5 pF, Z7 2.2 nH. Series: Z1, Z3, Z6, Z7. Shunt: Z2, Z4, Z5. Place all pads in the ground area, not the clearance area (AN 3.3 p.8); 0402/0603.
