# GNSS patch and SAW
## Taoglas GP.1575.12.4.A.02 (SPE-12-8-093-E; taoglas.com/datasheets/GP.1575.12.4.A.02.pdf)
- 12 x 12 x 4 mm (+-0.2) ceramic patch, 1575.42 MHz, RHCP, 50 ohm, efficiency 65.7 %, gain at zenith 3.21 dBic on a 50 x 50 mm ground (p.5-6, p.12 drawing), -40..+85 C. Passive, pin feed (0.7 mm pin, 2.4 +-0.3 mm below the body, 0.85 base), mounted with pin and double-sided adhesive (0.5 mm max adhesive). Mass not stated on p.1-13 (a 12 mm patch is about 1.7-2 g: weigh before MECH-1).
- Footprint p.15: top pad dia 1.2 over 1.0 hole (1.0 hole), bottom pad dia 2.2; p.16: 3.0 mm copper clearance round the pin on top, 3.5 mm on bottom side, all other layers like top; plane 50 x 50 mm recommended, antenna centred (our ground is 35 mm wide: gain will be lower, -1..+1 dBic realistic; the requirement/LC76G needs > 0 dBi: risk). Pin offset from centre (~1.0 mm toward -x, scaled from p.12 drawing, not dimensioned).
- Library: `dog_misc:GP.1575.12.4.A.02` (THT pad 2.2 / hole 1.0, front, hand or selective solder). A 12 mm patch is the source's low-cost option; the 15 x 15 sibling GP.1575.15.4.B.02 and Abracon APAE1575R1240ABDD1-T (12 x 12 x 4 pin type) are alternates (Abracon datasheet not read). Stock/price not checked.

Pads 0.2 x 0.25 (+-0.03), pad columns at 0.4 mm pitch, rows 0.5 apart (land: 1.0 x 0.75 overall, 0.25 tall pads). Insertion loss typ 1.1 dB (max 1.4) at GPS L1, T -30..+85 C. F7F vs F0A suffix (packing/ marking variant) NOT confirmed.
