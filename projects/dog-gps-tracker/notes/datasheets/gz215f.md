# CAP-XX GZ215F (datasheet rev 4.5, May 2020, pp.1,7,9-10; product guide v6.6 pp.3,12,13). Read as text + the p.10 drawing image.
Sources: https://www.cap-xx.com/wp-content/uploads/datasheets/CAP-XX-GZ115-GZ215-Datasheet.pdf ; https://cap-xx-assets.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/CAP_XX_Product_Guide_V6_6_7ba17eb07a.pdf
- Dual cell (two GZ115 cells in series). Abs max 5.5 V peak (2.75 V/cell), -40..+70 C (Table 1). Vn 5.0 V (Table 2). Our rail 4.0 x 1.03 = 4.12 V = 2.06 V/cell (82 % of Vn).
- C 72 / 90 / 108 mF (min/typ/max, DC 23 C); ESR 110 typ / 132 max mOhm DC 23 C (Table 2). C ~constant with T (Fig 6); ESR x2 at -40 C, x0.85 at 70 C (Fig 7) -> -20 C est x1.7.
- Leakage (Table 2): 1 / 2 uA at 2.3 V/cell, 23 C, 120 h (typ/max column reading; the table text prints "1 2"). Fig 10 (GZ115): typ equilibrium 0.5 uA at 23 C, ~5 uA at 70 C -> x10 per 47 C, x3.75 at 50 C.
- IRMS 4.5 A, Ipeak 30 A single pulse (0.5 A burst: far inside). Thickness F option 2.5 mm (G option 2.6 mm with tape).
- Size 20.0 (+1.0/-0.5 incl. terminals) x 15.0 x 2.5 mm; body 19.0 +-0.3 x 15.0 +-0.5 (p.10). Mass NOT published: Pgrav 83-121 kW/kg (guide p.6) with Vn^2/(4 ESR) = 56.8 W -> 0.47-0.68 g; use 0.7 g each.
- Terminals (p.10): 3 (1 -, 2 +, 3 BAL mid-point; schematic with balancing resistors R-R between V+ and GND, BAL at the midpoint). Tinned annealed copper tabs, 4 corner tabs + BAL tab at mid-left. Suggested pad layout (p.10): four dia 2.5 pads, 18.0 x 15.0 pitch (+-9.0, +-7.5); "customers determine final pad size". F option bottom: conductive copper contact 6.0 x 3.5 (3.0 from centre) at one side: KEEP CLEAR OF TRACKS AND VIAS. + marking on the face lower right. Which tab pair is + : read from a 110 dpi image, confirm on the first sample.
- Soldering (guide p.12-13): no IR / hot-air reflow, no wave; iron < 70 W, <= 5 s, 400 C max, hot bar, conductive adhesive, ultrasonic, laser. Ship discharged; solder discharged. Placement: no vias/tracks under conductive underside areas.
- Balancing (guide p.13): "CAP-XX recommends active balancing or passive balancing resistors" for dual cell modules, contact CAP-XX; the datasheet p.7 circuit shows resistors at BAL.
- Not in the text: ageing at 4.0 V/50 C (guide G series: <6 % C loss per 1000 h at 80 % Vn), cold capacitance (Fig 6 says flat).
