# TPS61299x / xA (TI) boost with average input current limit
Source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61299.pdf SLVSGS9G (Oct 2025), pdftotext 2026-10-31. See also library/datasheets/TPS61299.md (older notes).
- Family (sec 4): TPS61299 1.2 A; TPS612993 50 mA; 612994 100 mA; **612995 250 mA (Product Preview)**; **612996 500 mA** (production); 612997 1.9 A (Vout max 5.0). "A" suffix = force pass-through at EN low; no letter = true shutdown (<60 nA). Same SOT-563 DRL / WCSP pinout.
- ILIM (EC table): 612996 400/500/600 mA (Vin 3.0, Vout 3.5); 612994 80/100/120 mA; 61299 0.96/1.2/1.44 A. Average limit: output sags until power balance. 612995 limits not in the text (preview).
- Start-up (7.3.7): Vout < 0.5 V input limit ~20 mA; 0.5-1.8 V avg ~80 mA (peak ~350 mA); 1.8-2.5 V avg inductor current < 250 mA (612996); above 2.5 V back to ILIM. Vout < 1.6 V re-starts. In pass-through (Vout >= Vin) the limit is NOT active. Down mode when Vout < Vin.
- Vin: start 0.7 V (x) / 0.9 V (xA) rising; operates down to 0.5 V (falling UVLO). Iq 95 nA typ / 300 nA max into VOUT, 1 nA into VIN. Efficiency up to 91 % at 10 uA.
- VSEL (Table 7-1, p.14): 0 ohm = 3.3 V; 3.01k 5.5; 4.75k 5.5 fast; 6.19k 5.2; 12.1k 4.5; 14.7k 4.5 fast; 18.2k 4.3; 22.6k 4.0; **49.9k = 3.6 V**; 75k 3.5; 100k 3.2; 124k 3.0; 191k 2.5. 1 % resistor, < 10 pF on VSEL. Setting latched at start-up; toggle EN to refresh.
- Abs max: VIN, VOUT, SW, EN, VSEL -0.3..6.5 V (p.5). Vout range 1.8-5.5 V (<= 1.2 A parts).
- COUT: 4 uF to 1000 uF effective X5R/X7R (8.2.2.3, p.~26). A 160 mF supercap is OUTSIDE this range: keep >= 10 uF ceramic at the pin, bench-check loop stability/start-up. L 1 uH, CIN 2.2 uF.
- PSpice models on TI page: SLVME14, SLVME79, SLVME62 (not downloaded).
