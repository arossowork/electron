# Energizer E91 alkaline AA (files.batteryjunction.com Energizer-E91-Datasheet.pdf)
- Nominal IR 150-300 mOhm fresh (21 C). Operating -18..55 C (-20 C is below the rated range).
- 250 mA discharge at -20 C: CCV about 0.9-1.0 V in the first hours, against ~1.5 V OCV -> R_eff = (1.45-0.95)/0.25 = 2.0 ohm per cell, 4.0 ohm per pair (architect reading of the curve).
- Mass 23.0 g each; capacity 2850 mAh class at low drain.
