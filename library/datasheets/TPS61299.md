# TPS61299 (TI) boost, and TPS61099 (compared)

Source: ti.com/product/TPS61299 and TPS61099 product pages; HTML extract of SLVSGS9G (March 2023, rev Oct 2025) in stage 3 (2026-10-20). PDF tables could not be converted to text in stage 3.

## Stage 3 corrections (read before using the rest)
- Family table (SLVSGS9G, section 4): TPS61299 and TPS61299A = 1.2 A input current limit, 1.8-5.5 V output; TPS612997 and TPS612997A = 1.9 A (1.5-2.3 A), output 1.8-5.0 V; "A" = force pass-through at EN low, others true shutdown. TPS612991-6 = 5 mA-500 mA limits.
- Output voltage is set by a resistor VSEL to GND (21 options, 1 % resistor, < 10 pF on VSEL). VSEL shorted to GND = 3.3 V per a search summary of Table 7-1 (confirm). There is no fixed-3.3 V orderable code.
- Pins (DRL SOT-563): 1 VIN, 2 SW, 3 EN, 4 VSEL, 5 VOUT, 6 GND. Recommended: L 1 uH, Cin 2.2 uF, Cout 10 uF.
- Rds(on) 150 mOhm high-side, 88 mOhm low-side (VOUT 5.0 V, x/xA), search extract.
- LCSC: TPS61299DRLR C41689719, 3698 pcs, 1.898 USD (10). TPS612997YBHR (WCSP) C21713664, 4 pcs, 2.32 USD. TPS612997DRLR: not found at LCSC.
- PSpice models listed on TI page: SLVME14, SLVME79, SLVME62.
- Datasheet: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61299.pdf
